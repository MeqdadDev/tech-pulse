# TECH PULSE — SEP 08 – SEP 09, 2026

> Mistral AI raises a record €3 billion at a €21 billion valuation to anchor Europe's AI-sovereignty push, while XPeng's robotics unit banks over $900 million at a $6.3 billion valuation to push its IRON humanoid into mass production.

## AI

- **[Mistral AI raises €3B at a €21B valuation, led by Samsung](https://techfundingnews.com/mistral-raises-3-billion-series-d-samsung-21b-valuation-europe/)** — Europe's best-funded AI challenger says it now has over $1B in annual recurring revenue, positioning Mistral as the continent's flagship bet on sovereign, non-U.S., non-Chinese frontier AI.

- **[China unveils a $532B plan to roughly quadruple national AI compute by 2030](https://www.scmp.com/tech/policy/article/3366733/china-targets-fourfold-boost-ai-computing-capacity-2030-major-tech-push)** — the MIIT roadmap targets 9,800 exaflops of "intelligent compute" (from 2,185 today) and 100,000-GPU domestic clusters, an explicit bid to out-build U.S. chip restrictions with scale rather than cutting-edge nodes.

- **[Google says attacker AI agents compromised thousands of credentials in under six hours](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/autonomous-ai-agents-compromise.html)** — new Google Threat Intelligence research shows criminal groups now run largely autonomous, multi-agent frameworks across recon, phishing, and exploitation.

- **[Researchers use AI to build "WeWorm," a self-spreading WeChat exploit, in two days](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/08/wechat-weworm-vulnerability-exploit-account-hijacking/)** — the zero-click proof-of-concept hijacks accounts via a single missed call and could in theory have reached hundreds of millions of WeChat's 1.4B users.

## Robotics

- **[XPeng's robotics unit raises over $900M at a $6.3B valuation](https://www.therobotreport.com/xpeng-motors-humanoid-robot-unit-dogotix-raises-900m/)** — the funding backs mass production of its IRON humanoid, one of the largest single funding rounds yet for a humanoid-robot maker.

- **[DaxAI unveils a rideable, AI-piloted robot horse at the World Robot Conference](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/humanoid-robot-rides-robot-horse-in-beijing)** — the roughly $40K "Qiji X1" quadruped can carry two riders over 40km, opening a new category of legged robotics.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Google calls its EU-mandated search overhaul the biggest quality drop in its 29-year history](https://searchengineland.com/google-says-dma-changes-in-eu-resulted-in-worse-degradation-of-search-quality-ever-487340)** — complying with the Digital Markets Act, Google says some European businesses have seen direct-booking traffic fall roughly 30%.

- **[A misconfigured database exposes 220 million airline passenger and crew records](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/220-million-traveler-records-exposed-in-vietnam-linked-apis-leak/)** — nine years of passport and flight data tied to Vietnam-linked carriers sat open on the internet.

## Startups

- **[Stoke Space raises $1B for its fully reusable Nova rocket](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/private-spaceflight/stoke-space-raises-usd1-billion-for-fully-reusable-rockets-targets-early-2027-for-1st-ever-launch)** — total funding now $2.3B for a vehicle that hasn't flown yet.

- **[The Exploration Company raises $450M, Europe's largest-ever space-tech Series C](https://techfundingnews.com/exploration-company-raises-450m-series-c-bessemer-atomico-eqt-space/)** — scales production of its reusable cargo-return capsule, backed by $2B in signed contracts.

- **[Forus triples to a $3B valuation on a $150M raise for AI drug-access tools](https://dealroom.co/news/149379-forus-raises-150m-series-c-at-3b-value-for-ai-drug-access-tools/)** — AI agents automate prior authorization and medication access for pharma and providers.

## Hardware

- **[Samsung and SK Hynix warn AI-driven memory shortages could last past 2027](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/samsung-and-sk-hynix-warn-ai-driven-memory-shortages-could-last-until-2027-and-beyond-as-hbm-demand-explodes-customers-already-reserving-supply-years-ahead-while-the-wider-dram-market-begins-to-tighten)** — HBM demand for AI data centers is pulling DRAM/NAND capacity from phones and PCs.

- **[ASML and TSMC launch a plan to move High-NA EUV to 12-inch photomasks](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/tsmc-and-asml-announce-industry-transition-to-large-format-photomasks-for-high-na-euv)** — could lift lithography productivity ~40%, though it won't land until the early 2030s.

- **[Huawei's Kirin 9050 Pro tests "LogicFolding," a 3D-stacked chip architecture](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3366669/huaweis-new-kirin-chip-puts-logic-folding-test-bigger-ambitions-ai)** — claims a 55% density gain and 66% lower NPU power without cutting-edge lithography.
