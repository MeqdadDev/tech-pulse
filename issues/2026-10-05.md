# TECH PULSE — OCT 03–05, 2026

> Google launches Gemini 4 Argon to take on GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5, while Samsung moves to more than triple HBM4 memory prices for 2027 as the AI chip shortage deepens.

## AI

- **[Google's Gemini 4 Argon tops cybersecurity benchmarks](https://decrypt.co/379784/gemini-4-google-flagship-tops-ai-models-cybersecurity)** — Google rolled out its new flagship model first to cybersecurity defenders, posting a 77.9% score on the DeepSWE v1.1 coding benchmark and positioning it as a direct rival to GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5. The staged, defenders-first release shows how seriously labs now treat dual-use risk in frontier launches.

- **[Meta's Muse Spark helps mathematicians crack five open problems](https://alphasignal.ai/news/meta-s-muse-spark-helped-mathematicians-solve-five-open-research-problems)** — Meta published six papers co-authored with its Muse Spark reasoning model, five of which resolve previously open questions spanning probability, PDEs, group theory, and optimization. Mathematicians worked through Meta.ai's consumer chat interface to generate proof candidates and counterexamples, with humans still verifying every result — a notable real-world data point for AI-assisted research.

- **[Anthropic's bug-hunting AI finds a critical zero-day, exploited within a day](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/03/anthropics-super-bug-hunting-model-mythos-is-hardcore-good-at-math-as-latest-vuln-under-attack-shows/5300933)** — Anthropic's Mythos model independently discovered CVE-2026-61500, a session-forgery flaw in the Rejetto HTTP File Server; China-linked attackers then weaponized it within 24 hours of disclosure. A sharp illustration of how AI is compressing the window between vulnerability discovery and real-world exploitation, for defenders and attackers alike.

## Robotics

- **[Boston Dynamics redesigns the Atlas hand around four fingers, not five](https://www.therobotreport.com/boston-dynamics-drops-pinkie-on-new-humanoid-hand/)** — The new 13-degree-of-freedom hand drops the pinky, betting that "learnable, manufacturable" hardware beats anatomical realism for training humanoids via reinforcement learning. It's tuned for pinch, tripod, and power-tool grasps aimed squarely at factory work.

- **[California reins in robotaxis with a new safety law](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/04/techcrunch-mobility-reining-in-robotaxis/)** — Governor Newsom signed SB 1246, fining robotaxi operators up to $10,000 per vehicle that blocks emergency responders for more than 30 minutes and requiring U.S.-based, licensed remote drivers. Tesla, Waymo, and Zoox must comply by July 2028, after repeated incidents of driverless cars obstructing first responders.

- **[Agility Robotics partners with FORT Robotics on humanoid safety systems](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2026/10/04/agility-robotics-partners-with-fort-to-strengthen-safety-systems-for-digit-5/105492/)** — The two companies will jointly develop safety-rated control systems for Agility's Digit 5 humanoid, addressing a gap that's becoming a real bottleneck as humanoids move from pilots into warehouses and factories alongside people.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Google freezes its open-source bug bounty program over AI-generated "hallucinated" reports](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/articles/google-freezes-open-source-bug-120000127.html)** — A flood of invalid, AI-written vulnerability submissions overwhelmed maintainers' ability to separate real flaws from noise, forcing Google to pause new OSS submissions through at least Q1 2027. Linux maintainers and Intel have reported similar strain — a sign the "AI slop" problem has reached security-critical workflows.

- **[GitLab patches a critical AI Gateway flaw rated CVSS 9.9](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/gitlab-patches-critical-self-hosted-ai.html)** — CVE-2026-90970 let a logged-in user escape the prompt-template sandbox in self-hosted AI Gateway deployments and execute arbitrary commands. Only self-hosted instances running Gateway 18.1.6–19.4 are affected; teams on those versions should patch to 19.2.4/19.3.2/19.4.1 now.

- **[Anthropic ships "Mods" for Claude Code: TypeScript plugins that rewrite agent behavior](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-claude-code-mods-customization/)** — Developers can now write TypeScript hooks that rewrite prompts, gate tool calls, and change Claude Code's interface — a real step toward treating coding agents as an extensible platform rather than a fixed tool.

## Startups

- **[Schneider Electric nears $20B+ deal for industrial software maker PTC](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-04/schneider-said-to-near-deal-to-buy-ptc-for-more-than-20-billion)** — The deal would fuse PTC's product design, PLM, and IoT software with Schneider's energy management business — one of the largest industrial-software acquisitions of the year, and a bet that AI-era factories need design and energy data in one stack.

- **[Sean Parker rebuilds Stability AI around music, with label backing](https://www.cxodigitalpulse.com/sean-parker-rebuilds-stability-ai-with-a-focus-on-music/)** — After its generative-image struggles, Stability AI is pivoting toward AI tools for music professionals and working with rights holders rather than against them — a notable reversal for a company once synonymous with copyright fights over training data.

- **[Nebius acquires GPU-efficiency startup Inferize](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/byzggtiqgx)** — The $100–150M deal brings in a 10-month-old, 17-person Israeli team whose technology cuts the cost of keeping spare GPU capacity ready for demand spikes — a small deal pointing at a real cost problem every AI cloud provider is racing to solve.

## Hardware

- **[Samsung moves to more than triple HBM4 memory prices for 2027](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261005PD210/samsung-hbm-hbm4-2027-price.html)** — Samsung is reportedly pushing HBM4 pricing to the mid-to-high $4-per-gigabit range, versus roughly $1.50 for current HBM3E, with analysts expecting HBM prices overall to rise more than 100% next year. For teams budgeting GPU clusters, this is the clearest sign yet that AI memory costs — not just GPU prices — will shape compute budgets through 2027.

- **[Google's satellite-based TPUs hit 1.6 Tbps in orbit bench tests](https://thenewstack.io/google-suncatcher-tpu-satellite/)** — Project Suncatcher's early hardware tests of free-space optical links between TPU-equipped satellites support Google's plan to launch solar-powered orbital data centers starting in early 2027. A long-shot bet, but one more sign that compute providers are running out of room — and power — on the ground.
