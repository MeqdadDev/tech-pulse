# TECH PULSE — NOV 15 – 17, 2026

> Anthropic sets pricing this week for a Nasdaq debut that could value it at up to $1.4 trillion — backed by a $26 billion private placement from Nvidia, Google, and five other tech giants — just as Nvidia reports its first quarterly earnings since Jensen Huang's abrupt resignation as CEO.

## AI

- **[Anthropic sets terms for a debut that could value it at $1.4 trillion](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-ipo-terms-1-4-trillion-valuation)** — Anthropic's IPO is set to price this week at $70–$78 a share, implying a fully diluted valuation of up to $1.4 trillion; trading is due to begin November 19 on the Nasdaq under ticker ANT. A separate $26 billion private placement — led by Google, Nvidia, and AMD, joined by Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft, and TSMC — brings the total raise to as much as $90 billion.

- **[Nvidia heads into its first earnings call since Jensen Huang's ouster](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Sets-Conference-Call-for-Third-Quarter-Financial-Results/)** — Nvidia reports Q3 FY2027 results today for the quarter ended October 25 — the first release and call since founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on November 12 amid a foreign-intelligence probe. Interim CEO Colette Kress now has to convince investors the AI-chip giant can keep growing without him.

- **[Alibaba opens a two-week preview of Qwen4-Max](https://ai-tldr.dev/releases/qwen-qwen4-max-preview/)** — Alibaba's next flagship model is free to test through November 25, with a 1-million-token context window and strong internal claims on coding and agentic tasks over predecessor Qwen3.8-Max. No weights, independent benchmarks, or pricing exist yet, but it was the single most-discussed story on Hacker News this week.

## Startups

- **[Helsing backer Plural raises $1.1B to fund Europe's next moonshots](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/15/helsing-backer-plural-raises-1-1b-to-fund-europes-next-moonshots/)** — The operator-led European VC firm raised €1 billion, split between a third early-stage fund and a new "opportunities" fund for larger follow-on bets in energy, AI infrastructure, defense, and health — a sign serious money still flows into European deep tech even as US mega-rounds dominate headlines.

- **[Serena Williams launches Starfire Ventures, targeting a $200M debut fund](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/16/serena-williams-winds-down-her-vc-firm-launches-new-fund-targets-200m/)** — Williams and longtime partner Beth Ferreira are winding down Serena Ventures, which made over 100 investments, to launch a new early-stage firm backing AI-native consumer companies in fintech, health, and wellness.

## Hardware

- **[Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron face a federal antitrust suit over DRAM price-fixing](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/samsung-sk-hynix-and-micron-sued-over-alleged-dram-price-fixing-amid-record-memory-costs)** — The suit claims the three companies, which control roughly 90% of the global DRAM market, used the industry's shift to AI-focused high-bandwidth memory as cover to choke supply of older DDR3/DDR4 chips, driving commodity DRAM prices up roughly 700% over four years. It remains one of the most-discussed stories on Hacker News this week as memory costs keep climbing.

- **[ULA's Vulcan rocket suffers its first total mission failure, losing 40 Amazon satellites](https://spacelaunchnow.app/launch/vulcan-vc6l-amazon-leo-lv-01/)** — The Vulcan Centaur's return-to-flight launch from Cape Canaveral failed to reach orbit roughly 10 minutes after liftoff on November 15, destroying 40 satellites bound for Amazon's Leo (formerly Kuiper) broadband constellation — a serious setback for both ULA's launch cadence and Amazon's satellite rollout.
