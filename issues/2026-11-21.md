# TECH PULSE — NOV 19–21, 2026

> An Anthropic mathematician claims he and Claude cracked the 167-year-old Riemann Hypothesis, just as Anthropic's blockbuster IPO curdled into a $4 trillion AI-stock selloff that also caught Nvidia in an SEC accounting probe.

## AI

- **[Anthropic mathematician claims Claude helped prove the Riemann Hypothesis](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-prove-riemann-hypothesis-most-famous-problem-in-all-of-math/)** — Levent Alpöge says a six-week collaboration with Claude produced a 250,000+ page proof, formalized in Lean, of math's most famous unsolved problem and its generalized form. It's unverified and already contested (a rival human-only proof surfaced hours later), but if it holds up it would be only the second Millennium Prize problem ever solved with AI assistance.

- **[Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weight "looped" model that runs on a laptop](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/)** — The 26B-parameter model loops its middle layers instead of adding more of them, matching larger models like Qwen3.8-27B with roughly 5x fewer active parameters. It signals a real architectural alternative to just scaling parameter count.

- **[METR: AI capability growth is still exponential — and getting harder to measure](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/20/1149399/the-most-misunderstood-graph-in-ai-just-got-a-major-update/)** — The AI-evaluation nonprofit's updated "time-horizon" research finds models now beat humans on some week-long tasks, while still showing weaker judgment on open-ended, hard-to-verify work — a more nuanced read on where autonomous AI capability actually stands.

## Robotics

- **[US firm unveils Phantom MKII battlefield humanoid robot](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/us-foundation-phantom-mkii-humanoid-robot)** — Shaped by field trials in Ukraine, Foundation's updated humanoid carries a bigger payload and a tougher build for combat logistics work, underscoring how fast defense-sector humanoids are iterating from prototype to field-tested hardware.

- **[151 humanoid robots set a Guinness World Record with a synchronized dance](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/guinness-record-151-humanoid-robots-dance)** — Twenty ENGINEAI T800 units plus groups of Unitree G1 and AGIBOT X2 humanoids performed coordinated swarm control, a public showcase of how far multi-robot choreography and coordination have come.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[PostgreSQL 19 is generally available](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/19/release-19.html)** — The release adds online `REPACK (CONCURRENTLY)` for reclaiming table bloat without downtime, a `pg_plan_advice` extension to stabilize query plans, parallel-worker autovacuum, and near-2x faster bulk inserts via quicker foreign-key checks — a substantial operational upgrade for one of the most widely used databases.

- **[Critical OpenSSL buffer overflow disclosed (CVE-2026-121481, CVSS 9.8)](https://github.com/openssl/openssl/pull/33652)** — A severe flaw in `EVP_EncodeBlock()` is drawing heavy attention from security teams given how deeply OpenSSL is embedded across internet infrastructure; patching is urgent wherever the function is exposed to untrusted input.

- **[CrowdStrike reportedly in talks to acquire Zscaler](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/zscaler-crowdstrike-deal.html)** — A deal would combine two of the biggest names in endpoint and network security into a company with a market cap near $265B, consolidating cybersecurity platforms right as AI-driven attacks accelerate.

## Startups

- **[Anthropic's IPO — the largest in history — curdles into a market-wide AI selloff](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/wall-st-futures-slip-anthropic-debut-fails-reassure-ai-investors-2026-11-20/)** — Anthropic priced its IPO at a roughly $1.5T valuation, among the biggest debuts ever, but shares fell back toward the IPO price within two days of trading. The wobble helped drag the Nasdaq into its worst week since 2020 and reignited "AI bubble" concerns across the sector.

## Hardware

- **[Nvidia's stock craters amid a reported SEC probe into its accounting](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-secs-nvidia-probe-is-examining-potential-accounting-issues-sources-say-2026-11-20/)** — Shares fell for a seventh straight week (down roughly 44% in seven weeks) after reports the SEC is examining how Nvidia reported revenue, including the removal of Singapore from its geographic breakdowns — compounding a rough month that already included ex-CEO Jensen Huang's ouster.

- **["Frostbite": a CPU architecture designed and verified end-to-end by AI](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite)** — Architect Labs says its AI agents autonomously discovered a new general-purpose CPU design built on interaction nets rather than conventional instruction pipelines, reaching 4.59x the performance-per-watt of a modern superscalar core on a 25-workload benchmark — a notable proof point for AI-driven chip design.

- **[US approved nearly 900,000 Nvidia and AMD AI chips for export to Chinese firms](https://www.reuters.com/world/license-information-ai-chips-sent-china-released-by-us-house-committee-2026-11-19/)** — A House Foreign Affairs Committee disclosure shows Commerce Department licenses covering roughly 880,000 H200 and MI325X chips between December 2025 and May 2026, some to companies with reported military ties, intensifying scrutiny of US chip-export policy.
