# TECH PULSE — SEP 29 – OCT 01, 2026

> Anthropic's leaked IPO filing reveals a $2 trillion valuation target paired with stark existential-risk warnings and a $518 billion compute commitment, while Tesla secures $30 billion in new credit lines to scale Optimus and Cybercab production.

## AI

- **[Anthropic's leaked IPO filing reveals a $2T valuation target and existential-risk warnings](https://fortune.com/2026/09/29/anthropic-leaked-ipo-prospectus-losses-growth-ai-end-humanity/)** — Confidential S-1 documents show Anthropic seeking a $2 trillion valuation while devoting roughly a third of its prospectus to warning that its own models could "manipulate, blackmail, and exhibit other unpredictable behaviours." The filing also discloses $518 billion in planned compute/cloud obligations against revenue that grew 12x to $4.6B and an operating loss over $8B — a stark illustration of the bet-the-company economics now standard among frontier labs.

- **[Six frontier AI labs sign a White House AI safety accord](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/29/trump-top-tech-firms-sign-accord-to-self-police-ai-development)** — OpenAI, Google, Meta, Anthropic, Nvidia, and xAI committed to a voluntary pact involving external safety audits of their models, the most coordinated government-adjacent AI governance move yet, even though it carries no legal enforcement mechanism.

- **[Anthropic warns China's open-weight GLM-5.3 can build its own cyberexploits](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-claims-popular-chinese-ai-model-has-mythos-class-hacking-abilities-frontier-red-teaming-report-details-weak-safeguards-on-open-weight-ai)** — Red-teaming found Zhipu AI's model developed working Chrome exploits in 50 of 410 attempts (near Anthropic's own unreleased "Mythos" model) once its safety guardrails were stripped out, a reminder that frontier-level offensive capability is leaking into openly downloadable models.

- **[OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate nears $70 billion](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/29/scoop-openais-annual-recurring-revenue-nears-70b)** — Enterprise and consumer sales are both reportedly growing north of 70% year over year, underscoring how fast monetization is scaling even as the industry debates whether current compute spending levels are sustainable.

## Robotics

- **[Tesla secures $30 billion in new credit lines for Optimus and Cybercab](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/tesla-locks-30-billion-credit-020510298.html)** — The financing, arranged with banks including Citi and Wells Fargo, is earmarked to scale production of Optimus, Cybercab, and Semi, even as Elon Musk has separately admitted Optimus faces "extreme difficulty" scaling because "there is no supply chain" yet for humanoid robots.

- **[New industry data delivers a "humanoid reality check"](https://euronews.com/2026/09/29/five-million-robots-now-work-in-factories-as-humanoid-hype-faces-reality-check)** — Global industrial robot installations hit a record 603,000 units in 2025 (5.08M robots now in operation, led by China), but only about 7,000 humanoids were sold for real industrial/professional use — many bought just to generate AI training data — and Boston Dynamics is reportedly shelving IPO plans given its limited deployments and ongoing losses.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[GitHub brings its multi-model "HydraFusion" router to VS Code and the new Copilot app](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-30-hydrafusion-in-vs-code-and-the-github-copilot-app/)** — The system automatically routes each coding task to the cheapest sufficient model, a stronger reviewer model, or an independent critique pass, pushing AI coding tools toward adaptive, cost-aware orchestration instead of one fixed model per session.

- **[DoorDash launches an AI agent you can text to order food](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/doordash-launches-an-ai-agent-you-can-text-to-order-food/)** — The agent works inside Apple Messages, letting users place and track orders through plain-language texts instead of opening the app, one of the more concrete mainstream rollouts yet of a consumer-facing shopping agent.

## Startups

- **[ElevenLabs' valuation doubles to $22 billion](https://tech.eu/2026/09/30/elelvenlabs-doubles-valuation-to-22bn-with-300m-employee-tender-offer/)** — The AI voice startup completed a $300M employee tender offer that values the company at double its prior mark, reflecting surging demand for AI voice agents and another marquee valuation milestone for European AI.

- **[General Intuition raises $220M at a $6.2B valuation to build AI "world models" from gameplay data](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/30/startup-funding-news-today-september-30-2026-general-intuition-zenithon-arkeabio-more)** — The startup trains models on video-game footage to learn how actions change environments over time, a bet that games are a uniquely rich, cheap data source for teaching AI real-world physical reasoning.

- **[Factory's CEO accuses his own VC board advisor of spying for rival Cognition](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/factory-ceo-just-accused-his-vc-board-advisor-of-spying-for-cognition/)** — The AI-coding startup removed the advisor after alleging he leaked confidential information to a competitor, an unusually public flashpoint in the increasingly cutthroat competition among AI coding-agent startups.

## Hardware

- **[Micron posts record $54B in quarterly revenue as AI memory demand outstrips supply](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/micron-mu-q4-earnings-report-2026.html)** — Data-center revenue jumped roughly 11-fold on HBM demand, and Micron guided to a strong Q1, but also flagged tightening supply and raised capex — a sign the AI memory crunch is pushing prices and investment higher industry-wide.

- **[Researchers run a programmable quantum photonic processor in orbit for the first time](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/09/29/one-giant-leap-researchers-demonstrate-programmable-quantum-photonic-processor-in-orbit/)** — A University of Vienna/DLR team manipulated two photons through a six-path circuit aboard a satellite 317 miles up, proving a compact quantum-optical system can survive launch and operate in space — an early step toward satellite-based quantum computing for processing Earth-observation data on orbit.
