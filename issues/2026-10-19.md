# TECH PULSE — OCT 17–19, 2026

> Anthropic pledges 5% of its equity to a new public-benefit fund just as Disney moves to absorb the bulk of bankrupt Character.AI's workforce in a stalking-horse deal worth roughly $60 million a year.

## AI

- **[Anthropic commits 5% of its equity to a new "public benefit fund"](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/18/anthropic-says-its-committing-5-of-its-shares-to-a-public-benefit-fund/)** — (Oct 18) The wholly-owned fund will add another 2% each time Anthropic's valuation rises 50%, with CEO Dario Amodei calling public distrust of AI "a crisis of trust." Governance and spending priorities remain unspecified, with details promised in the coming months.

- **[Disney agrees to hire the majority of bankrupt Character.AI's workforce](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/18/disney-agrees-to-hire-the-majority-of-character-ais-workforce/)** — (Oct 18) The stalking-horse bid offers jobs to at least 60 of Character.AI's roughly 66 staff at an estimated $60M a year in pay, after the chatbot startup's Chapter 11 filing cited litigation and child-safety liabilities despite a large, engaged user base.

- **[Meta's new commercial terms open the door to AI training on scraped news content](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/10/metas-new-commercial-terms-will-allow-the)** — (reported Oct 15; effective Oct 30) Updated terms for Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp business accounts let Meta crawl associated sites and feed the content to "AI at Meta." Legal experts warn the language could be read to override robots.txt blocks, and business accounts have no opt-out before the deadline.

## Robotics

- **[An ex-Nvidia robotics exec says the field is still in its "Lego era," not its ChatGPT moment](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/17/an-nvidia-insider-turned-vc-on-why-robotics-hasnt-hit-its-chatgpt-moment-yet/)** — (Oct 17) Amulya Vishwanath, who led product marketing for Nvidia's robotics platforms and now runs Techable VC, argues basic building blocks like actuators, hands, and safety standards are still being rebuilt — pushing back on the idea that one general foundation model will soon make specialized robotics work obsolete.

A quiet window for robotics otherwise — no major hardware unveilings or deployments broke in the last two days.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[TypeSafe's Jev becomes Vercel's fastest-adopted AI model ever](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/16/jev-is-vercels-fastest-adopted-ai-model-ever/)** — (Oct 16) The lightweight "decision" model, built only to pick between a few predetermined outputs, hit 35% of paid teams on Vercel's AI gateway within two weeks — nearly double Sonnet 5.5's reach — and already has copycats from OpenAI and Amazon.

- **[Replit's CEO says the company dropped Anthropic for OpenAI — insiders disagree](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/16/replit-is-no-longer-working-with-anthropic-ceo-says/)** — (Oct 16–17) Amjad Masad cited cost and better internal benchmarks for vibe-coded apps, but a source told TechCrunch that Claude models were still live in Replit's production environment as recently as late September, leaving the real state of the partnership unresolved.

## Startups

- **[RapidCanvas raises $29M Series B for its enterprise AI ops platform](https://technode.global/2026/10/18/rapidcanvas-29m-series-b-vertex-ventures/)** — (Oct 18) Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India led the round, bringing RapidCanvas to $52.5M total raised, to expand its AI deployment and governance tooling into the US, Brazil, and India.

- **[Cursor parent Anysphere probed whether staff exposed client data to SpaceX before its $60B buyout](https://dealroom.co/news/163180-cursor-probed-whether-staff-leaked-client-data-to-spacex-before-60b-deal/)** — (reported Oct 16) Engineers flagged an internal tool that risked routing confidential customer data into SpaceX's Grok for training, weeks before the deal closed in August. No breach has been confirmed, but it's a reminder of the data-governance risk inside blockbuster AI acquisitions.

## Hardware

Hardware news was thin in the strict two-day window; the most relevant development still rippling through the market dates to earlier last week:

- **[China's CXMT spooks the memory market with a push into server DRAM](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/14/sk-hynix-sinks-5-as-chinas-cxmt-aims-at-server-memory-western-digital-drops-4-micron-falls-3/)** — (Oct 14) ChangXin Memory Technologies disclosed a denser chip design aimed at high-margin server memory, sending SK Hynix down 5%, Micron down 3%, and Western Digital down 4% on fears a serious Chinese competitor could erode the pricing power behind this year's AI-driven memory boom.
