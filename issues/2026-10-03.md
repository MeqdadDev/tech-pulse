# TECH PULSE — OCT 01–03, 2026

> Anthropic lines up $42B in Broadcom chip financing while pushing for a $2 trillion IPO valuation, and Boston Dynamics debuts a dexterous four-fingered hand built to teach Atlas real factory work entirely in simulation.

## AI

- **[Anthropic targets a pre-Thanksgiving IPO at a $2 trillion valuation](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropic-seek-2-trillion-ipo-161418318.html)** — Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows 2025 revenue near $4.6B (up 12x) against a $42B net loss and $518B in planned infrastructure spending. The filing, reported September 29–October 1, also devotes significant space to AI "existential risk," an unusually candid move for a company about to court public markets.

- **[Broadcom commits up to $42B to finance Anthropic's TPU chip leases](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/01/broadcom-to-lend-anthropic-up-to-42-billion-to-lease-its-own-chips-as-ai-circular-financing-hits-a-new-level/)** — The convertible-note deal, tied to Anthropic's $125.2B five-year TPU lease, could fund roughly a third of that commitment and may convert into Anthropic equity. It's the latest example of chipmakers financing their own biggest customers — a circular-financing pattern analysts are now watching closely for systemic risk.

- **[OpenAI ships GPT-6.1 Sol at one-fifth the price of GPT-6 Astra](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/30/openai-releases-gpt-6-1-sol-near-astra-coding-and-computer-use-at-one-fifth-of-astras-token-price/)** — Sol matches Astra on coding benchmarks and even beats Claude Opus 5.5 on AutomationBench at roughly a third of the cost per task, with a 1.05M-token context window. It's a notable step toward commoditizing frontier-level coding and agentic performance.

- **[Google rolls out Gemini 4 "Argon" to cyber defenders first](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-01)** — The security-focused release autonomously identifies and patches critical vulnerabilities, hitting 68% on security benchmarks; Anthropic separately flagged that Zhipu's open-weight GLM-5.3 shows comparable autonomous exploit-building ability, underscoring how fast offensive/defensive AI capability is spreading beyond the frontier labs.

- **[OpenAI warns 100+ organizations of unauthorized AI-agent activity amid a California AG subpoena](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026)** — OpenAI is reviewing roughly 50 petabytes of data tied to the incident while facing an investigative subpoena over cybersecurity practices — a reminder that agent deployments are outrunning the guardrails around them.

## Robotics

- **[Boston Dynamics gives Atlas a new four-fingered, 13-DOF hand built for sim-to-real learning](https://www.therobotreport.com/boston-dynamics-drops-pinkie-on-new-humanoid-hand/)** — The team deliberately dropped the pinky, judging the added complexity wasn't worth it, in favor of backdrivable, transparent actuators that make the hand easier to simulate accurately. It can already run drills, grinders, welding torches and nail guns — a clear bet that manipulation, not grasp variety, is the harder and more valuable problem.

- **[Germany bets on becoming the "parts supplier" to China's humanoid robot boom](https://euronews.com/2026/10/01/humanoid-robots-china-builds-them-but-germany-could-supply-the-joints-and-hands)** — With China building 97% of 2026's humanoid deliveries, German suppliers like Schaeffler (a new low-cost gearbox) and a Bosch–Schunk robot-hand partnership are positioning around the 40+ precision joints each humanoid needs. Fraunhofer projects a €30B component market by 2030, though access to Chinese OEMs early in the design cycle remains a real constraint.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[GitHub Copilot's "computer use" enters public preview](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026)** — Copilot can now read the screen, click, and type inside desktop apps on macOS and Windows after user approval, enabling browser-free automation of workflows that previously needed custom scripting or RPA tools.

- **[Claude merges Cowork and chat into one persistent experience](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026)** — Long-running tasks now keep working after you close your laptop, with projects and context carrying over between chat and Cowork — directly relevant for anyone running multi-step agent workflows day to day.

- **[Cloudflare open-sources Clef and Clef-flash, decision models that skip prose entirely](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026)** — Instead of generating text, these Apache-2.0-licensed models score allowed choices directly, hitting ~39ms median latency — a pragmatic architecture for latency-sensitive agent routing and guardrail decisions.

- **[GitHub's async merge API reaches general availability](https://github.blog/changelog/)** — Alongside dynamic workflows landing in Copilot CLI/app and an expanded Actions retention policy, GitHub continues steadily hardening its agentic and CI tooling (changelog, Oct 1–2).

## Startups

- **[PaleBlueDot AI raises $200M Series C for GPU infrastructure at a $3.2B valuation](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/02/startup-funding-news-today-october-2-2026-palebluedot-ai-certo-aerospace-drivex-more/)** — Led by ComputeCore with B Capital participating, the round funds dedicated GPU clusters, a third-party compute marketplace, and serverless inference — more evidence that compute-capacity middlemen are becoming a durable category of their own.

- **[Armadin Security raises $255.5M Series B for autonomous security agents](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026)** — Co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and Accel, the round pushes Kevin Mandia's offensive/autonomous-security startup past $445M raised at a $2.5B+ valuation, reflecting investor appetite for AI agents on both sides of the cybersecurity fight.

- **[Certo Aerospace raises £5M for an autonomous VTOL logistics and casualty-evacuation aircraft](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/02/startup-funding-news-today-october-2-2026-palebluedot-ai-certo-aerospace-drivex-more/)** — Its Capstone aircraft is a 600kg coaxial autonomous VTOL aimed at logistics and defense use cases, part of a broader wave of funding into autonomous aerospace hardware.

- **[SoftBank closes its final $10B tranche into OpenAI](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026)** — This completes SoftBank's $30B commitment, bringing its cumulative OpenAI investment to roughly $64.6B for about 13% ownership — one of the largest single-investor bets in the industry's history.

## Hardware

- **[Samsung hikes Galaxy S26 series prices by $100 as memory costs surge](https://thenextweb.com/news/samsung-s26-price-rise-memory)** — The S26, S26+, and S26 Ultra all got pricier (the 1TB Ultra jumped $200), a direct, consumer-visible consequence of the AI-driven DRAM and NAND shortage pushing component costs up industry-wide.

- **[OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast runs on NVIDIA Blackwell, with up to 8x faster token generation](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/gpus-openai-gpt-6-astra-ultrafast/)** — Nvidia says OpenAI is now using its own models to hand-optimize kernels for Blackwell, improving latency, throughput, and cost — a sign that model-hardware co-design is becoming a competitive differentiator, not just a research exercise.

It was a quieter window for consumer hardware specifically — most of the notable hardware news this cycle is really chip-financing and AI-infrastructure news (see the Broadcom/Anthropic and Nvidia items above).
