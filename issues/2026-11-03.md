# TECH PULSE — OCT 30 – NOV 03, 2026

> Anthropic's Claude autonomously found and exploited a critical Linux kernel vulnerability that Ubuntu and RHEL still haven't patched three weeks later, while Agility Robotics' shareholders voted this week on taking its Nvidia-backed humanoid robot business public via a $2.5 billion SPAC merger.

## AI

- **[Oracle files to sell $20 billion in stock and bonds to fund AI buildout](https://dataconomy.com/2026/11/02/oracle-files-to-raise-20-billion-for-ai-buildout/)** — Oracle filed to raise $20B — split evenly between common stock and senior notes — to keep funding its AI data-center expansion, a week after outlining the plan at its investor day. Shares fell on the news and are now down roughly a third this year, as investors keep questioning how much debt the AI buildout can safely carry.

- **[China's first frontier-AI export controls move into public comment](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-china-ai-export-controls-announcement-48-2/)** — MOFCOM's Announcement No. 48 would restrict exporting model weights above 10^26 training FLOPs or 2 trillion parameters, capable AI agents, and AI-assisted vulnerability-discovery tools. An interim registration requirement already applies to covered exports, while the full catalogue stays open for comment until Nov. 15 — a template that could reshape how Chinese AI labs deal with global customers and researchers.

- **[Anthropic files S-1 with the SEC for proposed initial public offering](https://anthropic.com/news/s1-sec)** — A week after formally filing to list on Nasdaq under "ANT," debate over whether regulators should scrutinize a roughly $2T AI lab's IPO — given open questions about how it discloses model-risk — was still a top Hacker News topic this week, a sign of how contested Anthropic's path to market has become.

## Robotics

- **[SEC declares Agility Robotics' SPAC merger registration effective](https://www.unite.ai/?p=485205)** — With its S-4 cleared by the SEC, Nvidia- and Foxconn-backed Agility Robotics put its $2.5B merger with Churchill Capital Corp XI to an extraordinary shareholder vote on Nov. 2 — the last major step before its Digit humanoid warehouse-robot business lists on Nasdaq as "AGLT."

- **[North American robot orders increase 24.8% in Q3 2026](https://www.robotics247.com/article/a3-north-american-robot-orders-increase-24.8-in-q3-2026)** — Newly released A3 data shows North American companies ordered nearly 11,000 robots worth $723.5M last quarter, with automotive-component and semiconductor-related orders both almost doubling. It's an early signal that industrial-automation demand, sluggish for years, may be turning a corner alongside AI-driven chip-manufacturing investment.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Linux kernel TCP flaw with 9.8 CVSS still unpatched](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/linux-kernel-tcp-flaw-with-98-cvss.html)** — Anthropic disclosed that its Claude model wrote a working exploit for a critical Linux kernel use-after-free (CVE-2026-98432) after only light prompting — leaking a kernel address in 48 minutes and gaining root five minutes later. Nearly three weeks on, Ubuntu and RHEL still have no fixed kernel available, a gap worth tracking for anyone running affected distros.

- **[Postgres 19 is late, and it's AI's fault](https://neon.com/blog/postgres-19-is-late-and-its-ais-fault)** — A PostgreSQL committer detailed how LLM-assisted bug hunting drove a 60–74% jump in back-patched fixes, forcing three major features (including SQL/PGQ) to be reverted late and pushing the Postgres 19 release into November. It's a concrete, unusually candid data point on how AI is reshaping open-source maintainer workloads — surfacing more real bugs, but also more review burden — and it's still generating fresh discussion this week.

## Startups

- **[Salesforce pushes further into defense tech via Rune acquisition](https://www.washingtontechnology.com/companies/2026/10/salesforce-pushes-further-defense-tech-rune-acquisition/416943/)** — Salesforce agreed to acquire Rune Technologies — an Anduril-alumni-founded maker of disconnection-tolerant military logistics software (TyrOS) — reportedly for around $375M, its largest national-security acquisition to date, folding Rune's tools into its Missionforce agentic-AI government offering.

- **[Ex-Palantir execs launch Dream with $17M from a16z to build AI world models for construction](https://thenextweb.com/news/dream-17m-seed-ai-building-design)** — Dream, founded by two ex-Palantir product leads, trains AI on more than 100 million square feet of permitted U.S. buildings to generate construction-ready architectural drawings — and is already architect of record on 10 real commercial projects. It's a fresh example of AI "world models" moving out of robotics demos and into regulated, liability-bearing professional work.

## Hardware

- **[Commodity DRAM prices hit a record as NAND's 21-month rally stalls](https://www.thelec.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=15148)** — TrendForce data published this week shows commodity DRAM (8Gb DDR4) prices rose again in October to an all-time high — the 19th straight monthly increase — as manufacturers keep shifting capacity to AI server memory like HBM. NAND's long rally finally stalled, but TrendForce still expects PC DRAM contract prices to climb another 13–18% in Q4, and Korean makers to cut PC DRAM output 20–40% in 2027 — a squeeze that will keep showing up in laptop, desktop, and SSD prices into next year. Outside the memory market, hardware news was quiet this cycle — no major chip, device, or infrastructure launches broke this window.
