# TECH PULSE — OCT 30 – NOV 01, 2026

> OpenAI slashes its in-flight funding round from $30 billion to $20 billion as Wall Street sours on AI infrastructure spending, while Tesla reportedly prepares to open $20,000 Optimus humanoid-robot reservations as early as December.

## AI

- **[OpenAI cuts its funding round target by a third, to $20 billion](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/31/openai-reportedly-lowers-funding-target-for-new-round/)** — The round, reported by the Financial Times, shrank from $30B (at a $1.4T valuation) to $20B (at $1.25T) after a sharp tech-stock selloff. A UAE investor group is leading; only about $11B is secured so far, and some large investors' response has reportedly been "tepid." Anthropic's stronger IPO filing is adding to the pressure.

- **[S&P cuts Meta's credit outlook to negative over its AI spending](https://fortune.com/2026/10/31/ai-boom-bust-openai-fundraising-target-meta-credit-warning-cash-flow-capex)** — S&P Global warned Meta's leverage could cross its downgrade threshold as soon as 2027, as capex climbs toward $225B in 2027 and free cash flow swings to roughly -$71B. The same report notes Alphabet's own cash flow worsened and its stock posted its worst day on record — a sign Wall Street is getting nervous about AI capex broadly, not just at one company.

- **[Mistral's 1-trillion-parameter "Le Chonk" model drops its open weights](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/mistrals-1t-parameter-large-4-slated-for-october-31-drop)** — Mistral Large 4, a 1.05-trillion-parameter, natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model (about 52B active parameters), is set to publish open weights on Hugging Face around October 31 under an unrestricted license. It's one of the largest fully open models yet released, aimed at giving European and other developers a credible alternative to closed US and Chinese frontier APIs.

## Robotics

- **[Tesla reportedly plans to open Optimus reservations in December, at around $20,000](https://sfstandard.com/2026/10/30/tesla-optimus-robot-waitlist/)** — A robotics investor who organizes the Humanoids Summit says Tesla told him reservations could open around its December 1–2 event, with most early units going to companies and shipping about a year out. Musk publicly denied the report on X and Tesla hasn't confirmed it, but Musk has separately said Optimus still has unresolved AI, design, and runtime issues he expects to clear by late 2027.

Robotics coverage was otherwise thin this window — most of the real action is still the pending Agility Robotics Nasdaq vote (Nov 2), already covered in the prior entry.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[A trojanized Claude Code extension on Cursor's marketplace hit up to 5,000 users](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/official-claude-code-vs-code-extension.html)** — Attackers slipped a malicious loader into a fake version of Anthropic's official `anthropic.claude-code` extension, distributed through Cursor's marketplace rather than Anthropic's own channels. It installed an SSH backdoor, opened a reverse tunnel, and exfiltrated host data before Cursor pulled it roughly 12 hours later. Anthropic says its own systems weren't touched — only Cursor's marketplace distribution was affected.

Coverage was otherwise thin for dev tools this window.

## Startups

- **[Serval raises $150M Series C at a $3B valuation to take on ServiceNow](https://fortune.com/2026/10/29/serval-servicenow-fight-series-c-3-billion-valuation-sequoia/)** — Led by Sequoia, the round triples Serval's valuation from January and backs its bet that AI-written code can undercut ServiceNow's costly custom-configuration model for enterprise workflows. ServiceNow's stock fell more than 26% after its own earnings, giving the upstart's pitch extra bite.

- **[Upwind raises $400M at a $3.8B valuation for AI/cloud security](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-upwind-raises-400m-at-3-8b-2-5x-its-january-valuation/)** — The Series C, led by Bessemer with Canapi Ventures joining, puts Upwind at roughly 2.5x its January valuation as it builds security scanners for AI agents and cloud workloads — another sign investors are racing to fund the "guardrails" layer as agentic AI spreads into production.

## Hardware

- **[Global smartphone chip shipments fall 21% as memory costs bite](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-memory-costs-help-sink-phone-chip-shipments-21-apple-gains/)** — Counterpoint Research says Q3 2026 smartphone SoC shipments dropped 21% year-over-year, driven by rising memory costs, longer replacement cycles, and cautious inventory management, with entry- and mid-price phones hit hardest. Apple gained share (17% to 21%) and fared better than MediaTek and Qualcomm — a sign the AI-driven memory shortage is now reshaping consumer electronics, not just data centers.
