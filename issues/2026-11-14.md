# TECH PULSE — NOV 10–14, 2026

> Google's Antigravity multi-agent systems crack decades-old open problems in math and systems engineering, while agentic-AI developer Mirendil raises the week's largest round at $1 billion.

## AI

- **[Google's Antigravity "/teamwork" cracks decades-old open problems in math and engineering](https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/)** — Multi-agent systems built on Gemini 4 models, paired with formal verifiers like the Lean 4 proof checker, proved information theory's 2013 Courtade–Kumar conjecture and produced a fully machine-checked formalization of the Weil conjectures (1.8 million lines of Lean, 28,800 subagent calls). The same tools rewrote Google's Zircon microkernel and AV1 video decoder into faster, memory-safe Rust and cut TPU workload preemption 24% in production — Google is now opening the toolkit to 10,000 academic researchers.

- **[Anthropic adds Sheets, Whiteboards and Meetings to Claude; OpenAI answers with a Business Hub](https://claude.com/resources/articles/more-ways-to-work-with-claude-sheets-whiteboards-and-interactive-connectors)** — Anthropic is pushing Claude into Excel- and Google Sheets-style spreadsheet editing, collaborative whiteboards, and an AI meeting assistant that updates documents live during calls — all sharing context with each other. Hours later, OpenAI launched a beta "Business Hub" letting companies build verified business profiles inside ChatGPT, the clearest sign yet that both labs are racing to become the default office operating system.

## Robotics

- **[DeepMind's Gemini Robotics 2.5 hits 96% success in real Mercedes-Benz factory trials](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-5-accelerates-real-world-physical-intelligence/)** — Google's most capable vision-language-action model yet was tested across 200+ distinct tasks — from tying bows to pipetting liquids — and ran an Apptronik Apollo 2 humanoid sorting automotive parts on live Mercedes-Benz production lines with a 96% success rate. DeepMind also open-sourced ASIMOV-Jailbreak, a safety benchmark for robots working near people, and credits Gemini 4 Argon's simulation abilities with speeding up training.

Beyond that release, it was a quiet window for new robot hardware launches; the bigger robotics news this period was financial — see Path Robotics' $300M raise under Startups.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[llama.cpp and its ggml engine reach v1.0.0 after years of development](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/v1.0.0)** — The open-source inference engine behind most local and offline LLM tools hit a stable 1.0 release: it locks down a long-term API, adds FP8 KV caches and a host-memory MoE expert cache, hardens its bundled server against SSRF and DNS-rebinding attacks, and ships a redesigned chat web UI. It topped Hacker News this week and underpins countless downstream projects, from mobile apps to edge-AI deployments.

- **[Claude Sheets, Whiteboards and Meetings vs. ChatGPT Business Hub](https://claude.com/resources/articles/more-ways-to-work-with-claude-sheets-whiteboards-and-interactive-connectors)** — Worth a second look from a tooling angle (see AI above): both products are explicitly agent-native rather than a chatbot bolted onto an old app, which is the more interesting developer-tools signal than the feature list itself.

## Startups

- **[Mirendil raises $1B Series A for agentic AI in R&D](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-startup-funding-rounds-agentic-ai-infrastructure-mirendil-mercor/)** — Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital led the largest round of the week for the San Francisco startup, which builds agentic AI systems aimed at research and development work — a strong signal that investors see "AI for science and engineering" as the next big agentic-AI category, echoing the Google Antigravity results above.

- **[Mercor hits a $20B valuation with $750M raise for human-led AI training](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-startup-funding-rounds-agentic-ai-infrastructure-mirendil-mercor/)** — The four-year-old platform, which connects AI developers with human experts for model training and evaluation, closed a Series D that puts its valuation on par with established unicorns — underlining how much of frontier AI progress still depends on human-generated training data.

- **[Torus raises $500M and launches Foundry, modular AI infrastructure for inference compute](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-startup-funding-rounds-agentic-ai-infrastructure-mirendil-mercor/)** — The Salt Lake City-based distributed power and compute manufacturer landed a growth round led by Magnetar Capital, aimed at the power and compute bottleneck behind AI inference rather than training — a sign investors are hedging across the AI infrastructure stack, not just model labs.

- **[Path Robotics raises $300M Series E to scale welding robots](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-startup-funding-rounds-agentic-ai-infrastructure-mirendil-mercor/)** — Georgian led a round that values the Columbus, Ohio-based "physical AI for manufacturing" company at $2.45 billion, extending the run of large checks into real-world industrial robotics rather than pure software.

## Hardware

- **[Google ships TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max, its most specialized AI chips yet](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/google-broadens-its-ai-hardware-portfolio-with-two-new-specialized-chips/)** — Co-designed with AMD, TPU 8tx builds confidential computing directly into the chip so companies can train or fine-tune models on proprietary data without exposing weights or data in the clear; TPU 8i Max doubles memory, compute and bandwidth over April's TPU 8i by combining two chips in one package. The launch lands as Microsoft, Amazon, OpenAI and Anthropic all push their own custom silicon to cut reliance on Nvidia.

- **[Amazon's Kindle Colorsoft gets a premium refresh](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/amazons-new-kindle-colorsoft-is-a-premium-e-reader-with-a-premium-price/)** — A smaller, more consumer-facing item worth a mention: Amazon's color e-reader line gets upgraded hardware and a correspondingly higher price tag — a reminder that not every window this period was dominated by AI silicon.
