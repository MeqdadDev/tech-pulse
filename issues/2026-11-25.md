## AI

- **[Anthropic mathematician says Claude helped crack the Riemann hypothesis](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-researcher-claims-claude-helped-him-prove-the-riemann-hypothesis)** — Levent Alpöge published a computer-checkable proof of the 167-year-old prime-number theorem, reportedly developed with Claude's help.
