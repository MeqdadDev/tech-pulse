# TECH PULSE — OCT 07–09, 2026

> Google turns Gemini into a full agentic coworker that can act across Workspace, Slack, and Jira for business customers, while Waymo closes its first-ever debt financing — a $5 billion loan from Blackstone, PIMCO, and Sixth Street — to expand robotaxis into new U.S., European, and Japanese cities.

## AI

- **[Google brings agentic AI to Gemini, starting with businesses](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/google-brings-agentic-ai-to-gemini-starting-with-businesses/)** — Gemini is becoming a unified agent that plans and completes multi-step tasks across Workspace, Slack, and Jira, delegating work to subagents and operating under its own workplace account with an audit trail. It can even route tasks to other labs' models, including Anthropic's Claude — a sign Google is competing on agentic capability, not just chat quality.

- **[Anthropic changes usage policy to ban model abuse and election interference](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/anthropic-changes-usage-policy-to-ban-model-abuse-and-election-interference/)** — The updated policy explicitly prohibits cruel, purposeless abuse of Claude in extreme cases (ordinary pushback and testing are still fine) and adds bans on election interference, deceptive-persona campaigns, weapons software, and surveillance — a notable tightening of the rules as agentic use spreads.

- **[OpenAI's revenue is reportedly $20 billion less than previously projected](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/openais-revenue-is-reportedly-20-billion-less-than-previously-projected/)** — The Financial Times reports OpenAI's real annualized revenue is "approaching $50 billion," not the ~$70 billion figure that circulated a week earlier (which used an Anthropic-style accounting method). The correction matters because investor comparisons between the two labs have been shaping AI valuation narratives all year.

## Robotics

- **[Waymo locks in a $5 billion loan from Blackstone and PIMCO to fuel robotaxi expansion](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/waymo-locks-in-5b-loan-from-blackstone-pimco-to-fuel-robotaxi-expansion/)** — This is Waymo's first-ever debt financing, led by PIMCO, Blackstone, and Sixth Street with Goldman Sachs as bookrunner, and it's earmarked to strengthen the balance sheet as the company expands into new U.S. cities plus Europe and Japan.

- **[AWS launches an open-source Physical AI Toolchain for robotics](https://www.therobotreport.com/aws-launches-open-source-physical-ai-toolchain-for-robotics/)** — Built with Nvidia (combining SageMaker and IoT Greengrass with Isaac Sim and Isaac GR00T), the toolchain covers the full pipeline from synthetic data generation through simulation, edge deployment, and feeding field data back into training. Being open-source and hardware-neutral, it lowers the integration cost for any robotics team wiring together AI training and deployment pipelines.

- **[Uber and China's Pony.ai plan to launch robotaxis in London](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/uber-and-chinas-pony-ai-plan-to-launch-robotaxis-in-london/)** — The partnership would bring Pony.ai's autonomous vehicles onto Uber's platform in London, extending the robotaxi land-grab beyond the U.S. and China into a major European market.

- **[Robot data startup Mecka AI raises $60M from Sequoia](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/07/robot-data-startup-mecka-ai-nabs-60m-from-sequoia/)** — Mecka AI pays people wearing body sensors to perform everyday tasks, then turns that human-motion data into training data for humanoid robots; Nvidia and Microsoft's M12 joined the Series B, betting that robotics needs its own version of LLM data-labeling.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Claude Haiku 5.5 arrives in GitHub Copilot](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-07-claude-haiku-5-5-in-github-copilot/)** — Copilot users get another fast, low-cost model option, continuing GitHub's push to let developers (and its own routing logic) pick the cheapest sufficient model for a given coding task.

- **[Local sandboxing for GitHub Copilot reaches general availability](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-07-local-sandboxing-for-github-copilot-now-generally-available/)** — Copilot's agentic actions can now run in an isolated local sandbox by default, addressing a real safety gap as coding agents get more autonomy to execute commands on developer machines.

- **[GitHub ships a purpose-built model for leaked-secret detection](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-07-purpose-built-model-for-leaked-secret-detection/)** — A dedicated detection model (rather than a general-purpose LLM) aims to cut false positives/negatives in secret scanning, a meaningful upgrade for any team relying on GitHub's security tooling.

## Startups

- **[AI leaderboard Arena nearly doubles its valuation to $3.1B in 10 months](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/popular-ai-leaderboard-arena-nearly-doubles-valuation-to-3-1b-valuation-in-10-months/)** — The company behind LMArena raised a $200M Series B led by Lightspeed and Khosla, up from a $1.7B valuation in January, and added an "alignment" category ranking models on unauthorized actions and false task-completion claims — evidence that independent, harder-to-game AI evaluation is becoming its own lucrative category.

- **[Vesta raises $30M as mortgage lenders adopt AI agents](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/vesta-raises-30m-as-lenders-adopt-ai-agents/)** — Vesta's agents automate much of mortgage origination, a process that currently takes ~40 days and ~$11,000 in labor per loan; the round (Conversion Capital-led, with Citi Ventures and a16z) reflects real revenue growth (12x YoY) even as Vesta holds under 5% market share today.

- **[North America's startup funding falls in Q3 as AI giants eye the public markets](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/q3-2026-north-america-startup-funding-falls-ai-exits-data/)** — Q3 funding dropped 35% quarter-over-quarter to about $92B, but that's almost entirely because OpenAI and Anthropic didn't repeat the $110B and $65B megarounds they raised in Q1 and Q2 — underlying venture activity is still up 50% year-over-year, with roughly two-thirds of dollars going to AI.

## Hardware

- **[Microsoft and Nvidia launch the Surface Laptop Ultra with RTX Spark](https://www.tomshardware.com/laptops/microsoft-and-nvidia-launch-surface-laptop-ultra-with-rtx-spark-rtx-spark-preorders-live-now-coinciding-with-major-windows-11-changes-for-agentic-ai)** — Starting at $2,599 (with a $5,999 RTX Spark Dev Box also available), the launch comes alongside Windows 11 changes that let Copilot take local actions inside sandboxed "Microsoft Execution Containers" — a concrete step toward agentic AI running natively on consumer PC hardware rather than just in the cloud.

- **[Seagate and Toshiba battle for TDK's HDD head business](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/hdds/seagate-and-toshiba-battle-for-tdks-hdd-head-business-a-critical-hard-drive-component-multi-billion-dollar-deal-threatens-sole-independent-supplier-as-shortages-intensify)** — Both companies are pursuing TDK's magnetic-head unit, the last independent supplier of this critical hard-drive component, as AI-driven storage demand intensifies broader hardware shortages.

- **[Taiwan indicts 10 for smuggling US military-grade chips to China](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/taiwan-indicts-10-for-smuggling-us-military-grade-chips-to-china-parts-routed-to-missile-and-radar-programs-using-forged-taiwan-defense-institute-orders-texas-instruments-and-analog-devices-hardware-passed-off-as-made-in-taiwan)** — Prosecutors allege Texas Instruments and Analog Devices chips were relabeled as Taiwan-made and routed to Chinese missile and radar programs, underscoring how porous chip-export controls remain even on supposedly restricted hardware.
