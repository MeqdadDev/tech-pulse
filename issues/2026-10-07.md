# TECH PULSE — OCT 05 – 07, 2026

> Schneider Electric signs a definitive $22.6 billion deal to acquire industrial software giant PTC, while Boston Dynamics names ex-Amazon AI chief Rohit Prasad as CEO to push its humanoid robots from the lab into real factories.

## AI

- **[Reflection AI debuts Beam, an open-weight model built to rival Chinese AI at a fraction of the cost](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/reflection-debuts-beam-a-open-weight-ai-model-to-rival-chinese-models-at-lower-compute-cost/)** — Beam is a 501B-parameter (23B active) mixture-of-experts model with a 1M-token context window that Reflection says matches Chinese open models like Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on reasoning benchmarks while using 3-4x less inference compute. It's a notable bid for a credible Western open-weight alternative in a space China currently dominates.

- **[OpenAI rolls out a new visual ad format inside ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/new-chatgpt-ads-format-and-measurement/)** — The format surfaces inspirational product imagery during image generation for Free/Go users, backed by new conversion-tracking and brand-safety partnerships. With ChatGPT now at 1.2 billion weekly users, this is OpenAI's clearest step yet toward building an ad business on top of its consumer scale.

- **[Anthropic expands its Cyber Verification Program for security researchers](https://cybersecuritynews.com/anthropic-cyber-verification-program/)** — The program now offers tiered access (Defense, Red Team, Specialized) that substantially loosens Claude's safety restrictions for vetted defenders doing penetration testing or critical-infrastructure security work, while keeping public access locked down — an attempt to let legitimate security teams use frontier capability without opening the door to misuse.

## Robotics

- **[Boston Dynamics names ex-Amazon AI chief Rohit Prasad as CEO](https://www.therobotreport.com/boston-dynamics-appoints-former-amazon-executive-rohit-prasad-new-ceo/)** — Prasad spent 12 years at Amazon leading Alexa and AGI research (and built the Nova model family) before nearly 14 years in ML research at Raytheon BBN. His mandate: turn Boston Dynamics' robotics lead into shipped, AI-driven commercial products for industrial and warehouse settings, following the company's new four-fingered Atlas hand and a new manufacturing training center.

- **[Industrial robotics startup RobCo surpasses a $1 billion valuation](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/05/robotics-startup-robco-valued-at-1b-in-40m-funding-round)** — The German maker of Alfie, a two-armed mobile robot that uses its "RobVision" system to adapt to production changes without manual recoding, raised $40M from Sequoia and Lightspeed in a round that doubled its January valuation — mostly secondary shares sold by employees, a sign of real liquidity demand around the company.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Schneider Electric signs a definitive $22.6 billion deal to acquire PTC](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/05/schneider-electric-ptc)** — One of the largest industrial-software acquisitions this year, the deal fuses PTC's product design, PLM, and IoT software with Schneider's energy-management business — a bet that AI-era factories need design, lifecycle, and energy data under one roof rather than stitched together.

- **[SAP expands its Joule agent into a full "Autonomous Enterprise" work layer](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-october-6-2026)** — The expansion pushes Joule from a chat assistant toward handling core business operations end-to-end, part of a broader wave this week of enterprise vendors (Microsoft Copilot Studio's new "Hooks" reliability feature, Meta Muse's small-business workflows) racing to make agents dependable enough for real operational use rather than demos.

## Startups

- **[AI cloud computing startup Lambda looks to raise up to $4 billion ahead of a planned 2027 IPO](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/ai-computing-startup-lambda-to-raise-4b-ahead-of-planned-ipo/)** — Led by Coatue and Blackstone at a $14.5B pre-money valuation, the round would fund data-center buildouts as Lambda's backlog surged from $15B in June to $50B in September, driven largely by its $35B compute commitment from Anthropic — likely Lambda's last private raise before going public.

- **[AI chip startup Etched is fielding funding offers at a $40–50 billion valuation](https://www.renascence.io/news/93242/etched-reportedly-fielding-funding-offers-at-40b-valuation)** — The jump comes just weeks after a $21 billion round, and analysts say it's being driven by actual customer orders for Etched's inference-specialized chips rather than pure investor speculation — another sign of how fast AI-chip valuations are moving this quarter.

## Hardware

- **[Intel's stock drops as TSMC reportedly eyes joining Musk's Terafab chip venture](https://qz.com/intel-stock-tsmc-musk-terafab-chip-venture-100526)** — Intel shares fell more than 4% after Elon Musk confirmed early talks between his Terafab project and TSMC, raising doubts about Intel's position as Terafab's sole named chip-manufacturing partner since April — a credibility hit even though Terafab generates no current revenue for Intel's foundry business.

- **[Samsung's HBM4 price talks with Nvidia and Broadcom reach their final stage](https://sammyfans.com/2026/10/06/samsung-hbm4-price-talks-enter-final-stage)** — With most of 2027's HBM4 orders already locked in, the remaining negotiation is purely about price — Samsung is pushing for more than double current HBM rates, and analysts now expect industry-wide HBM prices to rise over 100% next year as AI memory demand keeps outrunning supply.
