# TECH PULSE — SEP 25 – SEP 27, 2026

> Anthropic locks in an $11.6 billion, seven-year cloud deal with Akamai that could grow to roughly $20 billion and hands it a path to a 5% equity stake, while Microsoft rolls out a full Copilot overhaul built around a persistent "Autopilot" agent that acts across apps on its own.

## AI

- **[Anthropic signs an $11.6B, seven-year cloud deal with Akamai](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/anthropic-to-pay-akamai-11-6-billion-over-seven-years-in-cloud-deal/)** — the agreement can expand to roughly $20B and includes warrants letting Anthropic acquire up to 5% of Akamai's shares, another sign frontier labs are locking in multi-year infrastructure commitments with a widening circle of cloud and network providers, not just the usual hyperscalers.

- **[Microsoft rebuilds Copilot around Home, Code, and a persistent Autopilot agent](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/25/introducing-the-new-copilot-with-home-code-and-autopilot/)** — the overhaul splits Copilot into a personal Home hub, a Code builder for AI-generated apps, and Autopilot, an agent that keeps working on tasks in the background and can act across apps on its own. It's Microsoft's clearest move yet from an assistant you prompt to an agent that just keeps going.

- **[Google ships Gemini 3.8 Live with a real-time talking avatar in 97 languages](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-live-with-live-avatar/)** — the enterprise-focused feature lip-syncs and switches languages live, giving businesses a visual, speaking front-end for AI agents instead of a text or voice-only interface.

- **[White House asks OpenAI and Anthropic to delay giving new models to UK testers](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/white-house-openai-anthropic-uk-testers/)** — the administration wants US security review to happen first, before frontier models reach the UK's AI Safety Institute, a fresh sign Washington is asserting more direct control over how and when allies get access to the newest US models.

## Robotics

- **[Waymo now serves 500,000+ paid robotaxi rides a week across 15 US cities](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/waymo-is-scaling-fast-heres-what-the-fleet-data-shows/)** — ridership has roughly doubled in under a year, and the company is expanding the unglamorous side of the business, fast local vehicle servicing, that scaling from a handful of cities to thousands actually requires.

- **[Amazon commits $100M to a new Indiana robotics manufacturing facility](https://www.therobotreport.com/amazon-invest-100m-new-indiana-manufacturing-facility/)** — the plant will build hardware for Amazon's own fulfillment and robotics network, another sign the company is investing directly in the physical supply chain behind its warehouse automation rather than buying it all from outside vendors.

- **[Digit-maker Agility Robotics is exploring wheeled robot designs](https://www.therobotreport.com/agility-robotics-maker-digit-humanoid-exploring-wheeled-robots/)** — the humanoid pioneer is looking beyond bipedal walking to wheeled variants for environments where legs are unnecessary overhead, a pragmatic hedge against the assumption that every warehouse or factory robot needs to look human.

- **[General Robotics bets on modular "skills," not one universal robot brain](https://www.therobotreport.com/general-robotics-betting-modular-intelligence-not-one-robot-brain/)** — its approach composes specialized capabilities that can be mixed and matched across different robot bodies and tasks, an alternative to the industry's dominant push toward one giant foundation model for everything physical.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[GitHub Copilot adds Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Sol/Luna, and Grok 4.7, plus local sandboxing](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-25-github-copilot-weekly-releases-september-21/)** — the weekly release also brings OpenTelemetry monitoring, assisted approvals for low-risk actions in JetBrains IDEs, and VS Code 1.139 support for running agents inside remote Dev Containers, SSH, and WSL — incremental but steady hardening of agentic coding as a managed, auditable workflow rather than a raw chat window.

Otherwise a quiet stretch for major dev-tool news this cycle — nothing else cleared the bar.

## Startups

- **[Island and Cyera each raise $400M as cybersecurity leads the week's biggest rounds](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-cybersecurity-ai-health-island-cyera/)** — Island builds secure enterprise browsing tools while Cyera secures data across both human and AI-agent access, together the largest pair of deals in Crunchbase's latest weekly roundup and a sign investors see agentic AI creating as much demand for data-security tooling as for the agents themselves.

- **[Snorkel AI raises $350M for training data and evaluation tooling](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-cybersecurity-ai-health-island-cyera/)** — the round funds infrastructure that helps frontier labs build specialized training data and test environments, underscoring how much capital now chases the unglamorous data-and-eval layer underneath the model race.

- **[Dextr AI emerges from stealth with $6.7M for hospitality AI agents](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/exclusive-from-booking-calls-to-late-check-ins-dextr-ai-raises-6-7m-for-hotel-ai-agents/)** — the seed round funds agents that handle everything from booking calls to late check-ins for hotels, a small but concrete example of vertical AI agents replacing narrow, repetitive front-desk work.

## Hardware

- **[Meta debuts $1,299 tethered VR glasses and the Muse Charm AI wearable](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/mark-zuckerberg-1299-meta-vr-glasses-ai-agent.html)** — unveiled at Connect 2026, the glasses pair with a phone for lighter, more affordable mixed reality while Muse Charm is a small pendant that keeps Meta's Muse AI agent listening and speaking on the go — Meta betting its AI agent needs its own dedicated hardware, not just an app.

- **[Qualcomm ships the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 and Extreme Gen 6, built for on-device agentic AI](https://www.qualcomm.com/news/releases/2026/09/snapdragon-leads-the-agentic-ai-age-with-two-of-the-world-s-fast)** — the flagship mobile chips are pitched explicitly around running AI agents locally on the phone, pushing the agentic-AI race down from the cloud into next year's Android flagships.
