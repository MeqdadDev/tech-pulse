# TECH PULSE — OCT 11–13, 2026

> Anthropic sets an October 14 pre-IPO investor day and pushes its Nasdaq roadshow to the week of November 9, racing toward a near-$2 trillion debut before Thanksgiving, while DeepSeek leads a $400 million seed round for a new robotics AI lab founded by an ex-Tencent scientist.

## AI

- **[Anthropic sets an October 14 investor day, pushes its IPO roadshow to the week of November 9](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/innovation/article/344409/Anthropic-plans-mega-IPO-before-Thanksgiving-roadshow-on-November-9)** — the pre-IPO investor meeting (tomorrow) and a roadshow start pushed about a month later than planned now point to a Nasdaq debut just before Thanksgiving, with investors reportedly pricing the company at $1.8–2 trillion. It's the clearest sign yet that September's leaked prospectus details are turning into an actual public offering on a real calendar.

- **[TypeSafe AI hits a $7.5B valuation three weeks after launch, on an $870M round led by a16z](https://pulse2.com/typesafe-raises-870-million-series-a-at-7-5-billion-valuation/)** — founded by a ChatGPT co-creator, TypeSafe's "Jev" model skips conversational text generation entirely in favor of extremely fast, direct decision-making. The jump from launch to a multibillion-dollar valuation in three weeks is one of the fastest of the year, even in an AI market used to fast markups.

## Robotics

- **[DeepSeek leads a $400 million seed round for a new robotics AI lab](https://qz.com/deepseek-winverse-ai-robotics-seed-round-101226)** — the round backs a lab founded by a former Tencent scientist and marks DeepSeek's first serious move beyond language models into embodied AI, putting the Chinese AI giant in direct competition with Physical Intelligence, Figure, and other robot-foundation-model startups chasing the same "generalist robot brain" bet.

Coverage was otherwise quiet on robotics this cycle — no other major hardware, deployment, or funding news broke beyond the above in the Oct 11–13 window.

## Software / Dev Tools

Unusually quiet two days for dev tools: no major new releases, launches, or incidents broke through from GitHub, the big IDE vendors, or cloud providers in this specific window. Worth checking back next cycle.

## Startups

- **[Corporate-card startup Ramp hits a $60 billion valuation in a $1.85 billion round](https://thenextweb.com/news/ramp-60bn-valuation-1-85bn-round)** — led by Dragoneer and Thrive Capital, the round roughly triples Ramp's valuation from earlier this year. It's a reminder that fintech and spend-management capital is still flowing at AI-lab speed even as the very largest pure-AI megarounds cool off.

## Hardware

- **[Apple holds its first dedicated smart-home event, "Welcome Home," today in Tribeca](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/apple-welcome-home-product-recap/)** — the company is expected to unveil its long-rumored smart-home hub (widely reported as "HomePad") alongside refreshed HomePod and Apple TV hardware. It's Apple's most direct push yet into the smart-speaker-and-hub category Amazon and Google have owned for a decade.
