# TECH PULSE — OCT 29–31, 2026

> Anthropic's newly public IPO draws a "junk" credit-rating warning from Blackstone as its Nasdaq roadshow nears, while Canada and Nvidia back rival Cohere at a $20.5 billion valuation in an unusual government-backed funding round.

## AI

- **[Google launches Gemini 4 Carbon, a cybersecurity-first frontier model](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-carbon)** — The new model tops independent capability rankings and is tuned for long-running defensive security work, but Google is rolling it out only to vetted cyber defenders and U.S. government evaluators for now. It arrives just two weeks after Gemini 4 Argon, showing how quickly Google is now shipping specialized frontier models. Frontier-model news was otherwise quiet this window — most of the AI story these two days ran through dealmaking and infrastructure instead (see Startups and Hardware below).

## Robotics

- **[Unitree's revenue jumps 56% as humanoid robot demand surges ahead of its Shanghai IPO](https://digitimes.com/news/a20261029VL212/revenue-robotics-2026-profit-growth.html)** — The Chinese humanoid maker posted the growth across the first nine months of 2026, adding fuel to a STAR Market listing that has already pushed its target valuation into the billions. It's another data point that humanoid orders are starting to show up in real revenue, not just demos.

- **[Agility Robotics' $620M SPAC merger clears the SEC, with a Nasdaq vote set for November 2](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-nvidia-backed-agilitys-2-5b-spac-clears-sec-vote-nov-2/)** — The Digit-maker's deal with Churchill Capital Corp XI values the combined company at $2.5 billion and includes a PIPE led by Foxconn, putting Agility on a path to trade as AGLT. Filings also show the company booked just $174,000 in first-half sales against a $362 million net loss, a reminder of how far humanoid robotics still is from profitability even as it reaches public markets.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[GitHub formalizes Copilot Fusion as its agent-era model router](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-28-route-and-orchestrate-models-with-github-copilot-fusion)** — Fusion, the production name for the multi-model routing system GitHub previewed in September, now orchestrates both Anthropic and OpenAI models across coding tasks and adds a new Copilot code-review feature. GitHub's accompanying Octoverse data is the real headline: AI agents now open over 40% of all pull requests on the platform, up from 17% in March. Coverage was otherwise thin for dev tools this window; nothing else rose to the same bar.

## Startups

- **[Anthropic's IPO draws a "junk" credit-rating warning](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/anthropic-expected-to-receive-high-junk-rating-from-credit-agencies-following-ipo-report-says/)** — Days after Anthropic's IPO prospectus went public, a Financial Times report says the company is expected to receive a high-end speculative-grade ("junk") rating from credit agencies. Blackstone, which anchored a $63 billion deal to finance Anthropic's chip purchases, now projects a double-B rating rather than the investment-grade rating it expected before 2027 — a reminder that even a headline-grabbing, trillion-dollar-plus IPO doesn't erase the risk in financing Anthropic's enormous compute spending.

- **[Canada and Nvidia back Cohere at a $20.5 billion valuation](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-29/canada-nvidia-back-cohere-in-round-at-over-20-billion-value)** — Ottawa is taking a direct equity stake — reportedly up to C$1.6 billion — alongside Nvidia, with Cohere required to stay headquartered in Canada and invest billions in domestic compute infrastructure by 2030. It's a rare instance of a national government buying equity directly into a frontier AI lab rather than just funding research or compute.

- **[Baseten set for a $30 billion valuation in new funding](https://bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-30/baseten-set-for-30-billion-valuation-with-new-funding)** — The AI inference infrastructure startup's valuation has roughly doubled from its last round just months ago, underscoring how much investor money is chasing the "picks and shovels" layer that runs other companies' models in production rather than the model labs themselves.

## Hardware

- **[XREAL ships Aura, Android XR glasses with Gemini built in](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/xreal-aura/)** — The $1,099 glasses pair a 70-degree field of view with a tethered processing puck that keeps the headset itself under 95 grams, and ship in the U.S. in mid-November. It's one of the first real consumer tests of whether Google's Android XR platform, with Gemini as the default assistant, can make smart glasses genuinely useful rather than a novelty.

- **[PsiQuantum unveils an optical switch built for AI data centers](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/10/28/psiquantum-optical-circuit-switch-ai-data-centers/)** — The 8x8 prototype, derived from PsiQuantum's silicon-photonics quantum computing work, routes light signals between AI processors more efficiently than today's electronic switches, and samples are already going out to prospective customers. It's a sign that quantum-computing hardware research is starting to spin off nearer-term products for conventional AI infrastructure.

- **[Google's first space-based data-center satellite goes live](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/googles-first-data-center-satellite-is-online-and-writing-poetry/)** — The Project Suncatcher prototype, carrying a single TPU that generates up to 1 kW of power, launched this month and recently ran its first task: writing a haiku with a preloaded Gemma 4 model. The mission will spend the coming months testing how the chip handles radiation and thermal stress in orbit, an early step toward Google's long-term bet on networked solar-powered data centers in space by the 2030s.
