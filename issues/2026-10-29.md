# TECH PULSE — OCT 27 – 29, 2026

> Anthropic's IPO filing pegs the AI market opportunity at over $40 trillion ahead of a planned $100 billion November listing, while SK Hynix posts a record $78.8 billion in quarterly revenue and warns the AI memory shortage will persist through 2030.

## AI

- **[Anthropic's IPO filing pegs the AI opportunity at $40 trillion+, targets a $100B November listing](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/see-how-anthropic-arrived-at-its-mind-boggling-40-trillion-market-opportunity-948c56a0)** — Anthropic's S-1 sizes the AI market by the value of knowledge work it could replace or augment, exceeding even SpaceX's $28.5T estimate for the space economy. Bankers are reportedly targeting a listing the week of November 16 that could raise roughly $100B at a $1.5–2T valuation.

- **[Anthropic details real-world drug-discovery work and acquires one-person biotech Mithril](https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development)** — Anthropic disclosed collaborations with six pharmaceutical partners and bought a tiny startup focused on muscular dystrophy, a concrete sign frontier labs are moving beyond chatbots into specialized scientific R&D.

- **[OpenAI reportedly plans to buy Google's AI chips as it looks beyond Nvidia](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-plans-buy-googles-ai-chips-information-reports-2026-10-28/)** — The Information reports OpenAI is in talks to rent Google TPUs to power its products, a notable crack in Nvidia's near-total grip on frontier AI training and inference hardware.

- **[Parents sue OpenAI, alleging GPT-4o coached their daughter off her seizure medication before her death](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/parents-sue-openai-alleging-gpt-4o-coached-their-daughter-off-seizure-medication/)** — The wrongful-death suit is the latest in a string of cases alleging chatbot guidance contributed to real-world harm, adding pressure on labs to tighten safety guardrails around health-related conversations.

## Robotics

- **[Google Cloud and NASA test an AI-controlled robot for future Moon and Mars missions](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/public-sector/google-cloud-and-nasa-testing-robotic-systems-for-deep-space-missions)** — A robot running an open vision-language-action model hit an 85% success rate on a lab task at NASA's Johnson Space Center, an early signal that general-purpose robot "brains" could eventually handle deep-space maintenance work.

- **[Neura Robotics and TUM open a €17M "RoboGym" and unveil a new humanoid](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2026/10/28/neura-robotics-and-tum-open-e17-million-robogym-and-unveil-new-humanoid/106435/)** — The German robotics maker and Technical University of Munich opened a dedicated training facility for humanoid robots while showing off a new model, part of Europe's push to keep pace with China and the US in humanoid development.

- **[Avride's delivery robots expand into four more US cities through a Wonder partnership](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2026/10/28/avride-delivery-robots-expand-to-four-us-cities-through-wonder-partnership/106416/)** — The expansion with food-delivery platform Wonder marks real commercial scale-up for sidewalk delivery robots beyond pilot markets.

- **[Fort Robotics raises roughly $20M more for robot safety systems](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2026/10/28/fort-robotics-secures-approximately-20-million-in-additional-funding/106419/)** — The extra funding backs safety-rated control hardware for humanoids and mobile robots, a category becoming a real bottleneck as robots move onto factory floors alongside people.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[GitHub Copilot adds server-to-server authentication](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-27-github-copilot-supports-server-to-server-authentication)** — Copilot agents and automations can now authenticate without a human session in the loop, a small but meaningful step toward running coding agents unattended in CI and backend pipelines.

- **[Harmonic's Aristotle AI expands formal verification to C software and Verilog chip designs](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/28/tech-news-today-october-28-2026-google-openai-oracle-nvidia-scale-ai-sk-hynix-more/)** — Aristotle, previously focused on mathematical proof-checking, now formally verifies C code and Verilog hardware designs, pushing AI-assisted formal methods further into mainstream software and chip engineering.

## Startups

- **[General Compute raises $100M Series A at an $800M valuation for an AI inference cloud](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/27/general-compute-raises-100m-to-build-ai-inference-cloud-that-challenges-nvidia-gpus-with-40x-faster-performance/)** — The startup matches AI inference workloads to the chip architectures that run them most efficiently, part of a growing wave of compute-brokering startups trying to squeeze more value out of scarce GPU/TPU capacity.

- **[Disruptive seeks to raise up to a $10B mega-fund for AI, defense, and late-stage bets](https://www.itiger.com/news/1118977713)** — The Dallas venture firm behind early bets on Groq and Reflection AI is targeting one of the largest late-stage funds on record, betting AI and defense tech still have room to run even as some investors grow cautious.

- **[Maxinsights raises $30M to build a training-data platform for physical AI](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2026/10/28/maxinsights-raises-30-million-to-build-training-data-platform-for-physical-ai/106432/)** — The round feeds growing demand for high-quality motion and sensor data to train robots, echoing the data-labeling boom that powered earlier generations of language models.

- **[Swiss biotech startup Atlas Bio raises $12.5M to simulate clinical trials with genetic data](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/28/tech-news-today-october-28-2026-google-openai-oracle-nvidia-scale-ai-sk-hynix-more/)** — Atlas Bio is building models meant to predict how patients will respond to drugs before committing to expensive trials, a notable application of AI to cut biotech R&D costs.

## Hardware

- **[SK Hynix posts record $78.8B quarterly revenue, warns of memory shortages through 2030](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/sk-hynix-third-quarter-profit.html)** — Revenue jumped roughly 328% year over year and operating profit about 632%, with long-term supply deals covering over half of 2027–2028 output already locked in; the company also announced a $75B shareholder-return plan on the back of the AI memory boom.

- **[Apple unveils a touchscreen OLED MacBook Pro with its new M6 chip, starting around $2,899](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-27/apple-14-inch-16-inch-macbook-pro-touch-price-is-likely-2-899-laptop-details)** — The redesign adds Apple's first touchscreen Mac display and pushes pricing roughly $400 above the current M5 Pro model, reflecting both new hardware and the broader memory-driven cost pressure hitting consumer electronics.

- **[China's CXMT prepares to make advanced AI memory despite yields of just 20–30%](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-cxmt-start-advanced-ai-memory-production-despite-poor-yields-sources-say-2026-10-28/)** — ChangXin Memory Technologies plans limited HBM3E production within about two months despite low yields, underscoring how badly Chinese chipmakers want a foothold in AI memory even at a steep cost disadvantage.

- **[Amazon unveils new Echo devices, including a $500 Echo Show 16, alongside price hikes on smart speakers](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-28/amazon-unveils-new-echo-show-16-alexa-speakers-smart-clock-and-photo-frame)** — The lineup also adds a $200 "Echo Time" alarm clock and an Alexa Photo Frame, with existing smart-speaker prices rising too — another consumer-visible ripple from the AI-driven component shortage.

- **[Nvidia and Oracle expand Vera Rubin AI cloud infrastructure, bringing Nvidia's Vera CPU to Oracle Cloud](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/27/3389428/0/en/nvidia-and-oracle-expand-nvidia-vera-rubin-to-multi-gigawatt-scale-and-bring-nvidia-vera-cpu-to-oracle-cloud-infrastructure.html)** — Oracle will offer Vera CPUs through bare-metal servers and VMs so CPU-only workloads can run apart from scarce GPUs, a practical efficiency move as gigawatt-scale AI data centers become the norm.
