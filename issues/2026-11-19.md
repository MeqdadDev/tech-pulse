# TECH PULSE — NOV 17–19, 2026

> Anthropic completes the largest IPO in history, pricing shares at $83 to reach a $1.5 trillion valuation, while Nvidia delivers its first earnings report since Jensen Huang's abrupt resignation as CEO.

## AI

- **[Anthropic prices the biggest IPO ever, topping $1.5 trillion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html)** — Anthropic priced shares at $83, above its $70–$78 marketed range, raising $81.3B and valuing the Claude maker near $1.5 trillion, surpassing SpaceX's prior record as the largest public offering ever. Trading begins under ticker ANT.

- **[Google ships Gemini 4 Neon, a faster mid-tier model](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/)** — Neon slots between Carbon and Hydrogen, ranks #2 on the Agent Arena Index, and reportedly beats Claude Opus 5.5 on coding at roughly half the cost — Google's fourth Gemini 4-family release in under six weeks.

- **[Google also rolls out Genie 4, its interactive "world model"](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/)** — AI Ultra subscribers can generate explorable 3D worlds from a text prompt and edit them live mid-session, with native audio and a new agent that helps build and populate scenes.

- **[Gemini's agent, renamed "Tasks," goes free and starts spending your money](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/tasks-in-gemini-free-new-features/)** — Google made its task-completing agent free in six countries and, in the US, gave it the ability to call businesses on your behalf and checkout with Google Pay within a spending limit you set.

- **[A broad coalition accuses Anthropic of "reckless" AI racing](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261118974464/en/Anthropic-is-Not-a-Safe-AI-Company---Broad-Coalition-of-Scientists-Legal-Scholars-and-Tech-Leaders-Challenge-Anthropics-Safety-Branding-Ahead-of-IPO)** — Timed to land the day of Anthropic's IPO pricing, the "MisAnthropic Statement" — signed by figures spanning AI-safety advocates to accelerationists — argues Anthropic's safety branding masks a race toward recursive self-improvement.

## Robotics

Little major robotics news broke specifically in this 48-hour window. The closest adjacent developments were Nvidia's automotive-and-robotics revenue update inside its Q3 earnings (see Hardware) and OpenAI's chip-design work with AMD, which touches the compute stack robotics increasingly depends on.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Hackers hijack Microsoft's official AI agent package on PyPI](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/microsofts-agent-framework-pypi-package-compromised-to-steal-credentials/)** — Two malicious versions of Microsoft's `agent-framework` package (downloaded ~200k times/month) were published with a credential stealer that ran automatically and could exfiltrate Azure and CI secrets, even without being imported.

- **[Critical NGINX flaws threaten millions of sites](https://www.wiz.io/blog/nginx-http2-rce-vulnerabilities)** — Wiz and AI-focused security firm Xint independently found five NGINX HTTP/2 vulnerabilities, including one exploitable from a default config for unauthenticated remote code execution — patch immediately if you run NGINX.

- **[Microsoft Ignite 2026 opens in San Francisco](https://ignite.microsoft.com/en-US)** — Satya Nadella and Judson Althoff kicked off the Nov 17–20 conference under a "Frontier Firm" theme centered on embedding Copilot and autonomous agents into enterprise data and workflows, with a full Book of News expected through the week.

## Startups

- **[Palo Alto Networks buys Applied Compute for $4.5B](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/palo-alto-networks-to-buy-ai-startup-applied-compute-for-4-5-billion-39193611)** — The cybersecurity giant is acquiring the year-old, ex-OpenAI-founded startup that customizes open-weight AI models — a huge markup from the $3.25B valuation Applied Compute had just this month — alongside a Q1 earnings beat.

- **[Bret Taylor's Sierra nears a $30B valuation](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/bret-taylors-sierra-talks-raise-30-billion-valuation)** — The AI customer-support startup, which said in October it had passed $400M in annualized revenue, is reportedly in talks to raise ~$1.5B, nearly doubling its valuation from $15.8B in May.

- **[Paris's Remanence raises $30M seed](https://ventureburn.com/remanence-raises-30-million-companies-train-own-ai-models/)** — The startup, which helps companies fine-tune and own their open-weight models rather than depend on closed-model APIs, closed the round led by EQT Ventures and FirstMark.

## Hardware

- **[Nvidia reports its first earnings since Jensen Huang's ouster](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/18/2026/nvidia-shares-fall-despite-bumper-earnings)** — Nvidia posted strong Q3 FY2027 results, but shares fell on uncertainty about life after Huang, who resigned Nov 11 amid an affair scandal. Interim CEO Colette Kress held her first all-hands the next day, moving up pay raises and equity grants to retain staff.

- **[OpenAI says its models are now improving the chips that run them](https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models/)** — OpenAI reports its models found up to 14% power/performance/area gains on AMD's flagship GPU IP — roughly a year of engineering progress in under five months — with some changes already sent to manufacturing, plus similar gains on OpenAI's own Jalapeño inference chip.

- **[Washington tightens the chip-export noose; China feels it](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/11/19/chinese-web-giant-tencent-says-new-us-gpu-ban-sets-everyone-back-in-china/)** — New US rules restricting China's remote access to overseas GPU data centers prompted Tencent's president to say the curbs will delay the company's next AI model and "set everyone back in China," underscoring how compute access remains the industry's chokepoint.
