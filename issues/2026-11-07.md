# TECH PULSE — NOV 05 – NOV 07, 2026

> OpenAI reportedly raises $11 billion more at a $1.25 trillion valuation, while Agility Robotics becomes the first pure-play humanoid robot company to go public on Nasdaq.

## AI

- **[Google DeepMind's AI agents expose two weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA encryption](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/google-deepmind-agents-expose-older-encryption-flaws)** — Working under human supervision, DeepMind's agents found a padding weakness affecting RSA signatures and a new factoring approach claimed to be roughly 1,600x faster than the prior best attack, both specific to old 1024-bit keys. Outside reporting is clear that modern 2048-bit-and-larger encryption — used for banking, messaging, and software updates — is unaffected; this targets only legacy, already-deprecated keys.

- **[OpenAI released a new cybersecurity model. Is that enough?](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/openai-released-a-new-cybersecurity-model-is-that-enough/)** — GPT-6 Cyber is tuned for vulnerability research and has reportedly surfaced 81 Firefox flaws and 948 Linux exploits during testing, with guardrails meant to keep it inside authorized "rules of engagement." It's OpenAI's clearest bet yet that specialized models can outpace human red teams — and a reminder the same capability cuts both ways.

- **[Hundreds of Artex AI hacking tools found publicly exposed to the web](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/hundreds-of-artex-ai-hacking-tools-found-publicly-exposed-to-the-web/)** — Censys found over 800 unsecured deployments of Artex, an open-source autonomous pentesting agent framework tied to real attacks on South Korean finance firms, ConnectWise, and Microsoft cloud customers. It's a sharp illustration of how fast agentic offensive-security tooling is spreading, and how easily it leaks into the wrong hands when left unauthenticated.

- **[Ramp data shows AI spending stalling just as Anthropic nears its IPO](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/ramp-data-shows-ai-spending-stalling-just-as-anthropic-nears-its-ipo/)** — Business token spend grew only 1.5% across August–September despite rising usage, suggesting price competition between OpenAI and Anthropic is capping revenue growth even as demand keeps climbing. It's an early, concrete signal that enterprise AI spend may not scale as fast as current valuations assume.

## Robotics

- **[Agility is the first pure humanoid robot company on the stock market](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/agility-is-the-first-pure-humanoid-robot-company-on-the-stock-market-are-you-buying/)** — Agility began trading on Nasdaq as AGLT via a SPAC merger, raising $465 million. Its Digit robots currently do narrow tote-handling work for nine customers, with H1 2026 revenue of just $174,000 against a $362 million loss — making this the first real market test of whether humanoid-robot economics can work at scale.

- **[Zipline hits $21.5B valuation as Walmart expands bet on drone delivery](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/zipline-hits-21-5b-valuation-as-walmart-expands-bet-on-drone-delivery/)** — Zipline raised $1.5 billion and signed a deal to grow its US Walmart drone-delivery sites from 21 to over 300. Its share of Walmart's delivery revenue reportedly jumped from 10.8% to 34.9% between March and October — a sign autonomous drone logistics is moving from pilot to real scale.

- **[Serve Robotics launches new business to become the AWS for robots](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/serve-robotics-launches-new-business-to-become-the-aws-for-robots/)** — The sidewalk-delivery robot company is now selling its connectivity, data-curation, and foundation-model-data tooling to other robot builders, with construction-autonomy startup Bedrock Robotics as a named customer. It's a bet that the infrastructure behind one robot fleet can become a platform for everyone else's.

- **[Physicl raises $10M to make the digital world real enough for robots](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/physicl-raises-10m-to-make-the-digital-world-real-enough-for-robots/)** — The seed-stage startup's Studio product offers 10,000 3D environments and 100,000 assets to generate synthetic training data for hard robotic edge cases. It's a small round, but it targets a real bottleneck: most humanoid and mobile-robot training still can't get enough realistic, diverse data.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Dropbox acquires Tigris Data for $350M to bolster its AI infrastructure](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/dropbox-acquires-tigris-data-for-350m-to-bolster-its-ai-infrastructure/)** — Reportedly Dropbox's largest acquisition ever, the deal brings in distributed storage built by former Uber infrastructure engineers to power DBX Agent Cloud, a new S3-compatible object-storage product aimed at developers building AI agents. It shows a legacy file-sync company pivoting hard toward agent infrastructure.

- **["The Duo apps are coming," says Apple](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/the-duo-apps-are-coming-says-apple/)** — Apple says over 100,000 apps are now live for its iPhone Duo foldable, with Word, Outlook, and Zoom among the latest optimized launches. It's a fast-moving developer ecosystem adapting to a new foldable form factor, even as the $1,999 device stays sold out with ship times slipping into 2027.

## Startups

- **[OpenAI reportedly raises an additional $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/openai-reportedly-raises-an-additional-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation/)** — The round, which could grow to roughly $20 billion, sets a fresh benchmark for frontier-lab valuations — and landed hours after reports that rival Anthropic's expected IPO valuation had slipped as low as $1.1 trillion from around $2 trillion.

- **[Palo Alto Networks in talks to buy AI agent security startup Clutch for $500M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/palo-alto-networks-in-talks-to-buy-ai-agent-security-startup-clutch-for-500m/)** — Clutch, which controls what company systems AI agents can access, was valued at just $85 million in January 2025 and is nearing $10 million in ARR — meaning the reported price is roughly 50x revenue. It's a striking data point on how urgently the market is pricing "who watches the AI agents" risk.

- **[Sequoia shutting down its Scouts program marks the end of an era](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/sequoia-shutting-down-its-scouts-program-marks-the-end-of-an-era/)** — As part of a broader pullback that also closes its London office, Sequoia is ending the founder/operator-run scout-check model it pioneered. It's a signal that even top-tier VC firms are re-concentrating resources rather than spreading bets thin in the current AI-dominated fundraising climate.

## Hardware

- **[Tesla will stop making the Cybertruck in 2027](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/tesla-will-stop-making-the-cybertruck-in-2027/)** — Musk said Tesla will end Cybertruck production next year, after it sold only a fraction of his once-predicted ~250,000/year pace, and will repurpose the Texas factory space for two-seater Cybercab robotaxis. It's the third Tesla product line — after the Model S and Model X — sacrificed to free up manufacturing capacity for robotaxis and Optimus humanoid robots.

- **[Pentagon offers battery startup 6K $250M loan to build U.S. factory](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/pentagon-offers-battery-startup-6k-250m-loan-to-build-u-s-factory/)** — The 12-year-old startup uses a microwave-plasma process to simplify cathode-material manufacturing across battery chemistries, including LFP. The conditionally approved loan is part of a broader defense push to build domestic battery supply chains that don't depend on Chinese suppliers.
