# TECH PULSE — SEP 21 – SEP 23, 2026

> OpenAI's math-solving AI draws a public rebuke from 25 Fields Medalists and a new Princeton oversight board, the same week Alibaba unveils its most powerful AI chip yet and plans a 10-trillion-parameter Qwen 5 model in pursuit of superintelligence.

## AI

- **[OpenAI forms a math advisory group as its AI resolves 100+ open problems](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/openai-forms-math-advisory-group-as-its-ai-resolves-more-than-100-open-problems/)** — on top of its earlier Navier–Stokes claim, OpenAI says an internal model has now resolved more than 100 open mathematics problems, prompting 25 Fields Medalists to warn that AI labs are "threatening their intellectual work." OpenAI's response, a Princeton-hosted advisory group, has no say over how fast the company actually publishes results.

- **[Alibaba unveils the Zhenwu V900, its most powerful AI chip yet](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/gemini/articles/zhenwu-v900-alibaba-most-powerful-124500673.html)** — it triples the performance of its predecessor and clusters up to 500,000 chips, while Alibaba says its next flagship model, Qwen 5, will scale to 5–10 trillion parameters, more than triple today's Qwen 3.8-Max, as part of an explicit push toward artificial superintelligence.

- **[Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5.5 at a 40% lower price](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-launches-opus-55-its-first-model-since-ceo-amodei-called-for-ai-slowdown-163000869.html)** — the new model matches Opus 5.1's performance at $4/$20 per million input/output tokens versus $5/$25 before, arriving just ten days after CEO Dario Amodei publicly called on the industry to "pace the frontier" — a reminder that competitive pressure keeps pushing labs to ship even amid their own safety rhetoric.

## Robotics

- **[NVIDIA releases Isaac ROS 5.0 with agentic AI workflows](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/isaac-ros-5-0-agentic-open-source-robotics/)** — the open-source robotics framework update adds AI-agent capabilities and expanded hardware support, a step toward treating "agents that control robots" as a standard developer building block rather than a research project.

- **[Boston Dynamics opens a dedicated Atlas training center at Hyundai's Georgia Metaplant](https://bostondynamics.com/news/boston-dynamics-opens-robotics-metaplant-application-center-to-train-humanoid-robots-for-manufacturing-tasks/)** — the Metaplant Application Center prepares Atlas humanoids for real assembly-line work, moving the robot from lab demos toward an actual industrial deployment pipeline.

- **[Robotiq open-sources its Physical AI software stack](https://www.roboticstomorrow.com/news/2026/09/22/robotiq-releases-open-source-software-packages-for-the-physical-ai-ecosystem/27136)** — a C++ SDK, ROS 2 package, and NVIDIA Isaac Sim assets for its grippers, the first pieces of a "Contact Core" software layer, let developers train and simulate manipulation models on real gripper hardware instead of relying on informally maintained community drivers.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Meta hot-fixes a Muse zero-day that let attackers hijack the AI agent](https://www.unite.ai/meta-hot-fixes-muse-zero-day-that-let-attackers-hijack-the-ai-agent/)** — a researcher found an undocumented dictation-endpoint setting that let a local attacker steal audio and prompts and remotely control Muse across a user's other devices; Meta shipped a fix within about a day of disclosure.

- **[Anthropic and OpenAI pivot to smaller, faster data centers](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/data-centers/openai-and-anthropic-are-reportedly-seeking-out-smaller-data-center-deals-to-meet-current-demand-20-30-mw-facilities-to-provide-capacity-as-mega-structures-undergo-construction)** — with gigawatt-scale mega-projects still under construction, both labs are reportedly scouting 20–30MW facilities to get inference capacity online sooner, a sign the AI infrastructure race is as much about speed of delivery as raw scale.

## Startups

- **[HighLife raises over $90M for transcatheter mitral valve replacement](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/22/startup-funding-news-today-september-22-2026-benford-cellpoint-highlife-bci-sonics-spott-more/)** — the Paris medtech's round is one of the week's larger deals and a reminder that serious capital is still flowing into hardware-heavy health devices, not just software.

- **[BCI-Sonics raises roughly $30M for a non-invasive, ultrasound-based brain-computer interface](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/22/startup-funding-news-today-september-22-2026-benford-cellpoint-highlife-bci-sonics-spott-more/)** — the Shanghai startup uses ultrasound neuromodulation and neural decoding instead of implants, part of China's fast-growing push into brain-computer interfaces.

- **[Spott raises $21M for AI-native recruiting](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/22/startup-funding-news-today-september-22-2026-benford-cellpoint-highlife-bci-sonics-spott-more/)** — the Belgian platform's revenue grew tenfold since early 2026 as it consolidates applicant tracking, candidate search, and agency workflows into one AI-driven tool.

## Hardware

- **[AMD becomes the fifth chipmaker to hit a $1 trillion market cap](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/amd-stock-1-trillion-value.html)** — its stock has nearly tripled in 2026 on AI-driven demand, passing Intel's valuation by roughly $350B and underscoring how concentrated the AI rally remains in a handful of semiconductor names.

- **[DRAM prices now exceed leading-edge compute chip prices per area](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/dram/dram-is-now-more-expensive-than-compute-chips-on-per-area-basis-ai-demand-drives-memory-die-value-past-leading-edge-silicon)** — AI-driven memory demand has pushed DRAM past the per-area cost of TSMC's most advanced logic chips, with forecasts suggesting DRAM supply could tighten by over 70% in 2027, a squeeze already showing up in PC and server prices.

- **[China demonstrates working 3nm-class transistors without EUV lithography](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/china-crafts-working-3nm-gate-all-around-transistors-without-euv-stacked-nanosheets-target-3nm-without-euv-but-full-node-remains-distant)** — Chinese researchers built functional gate-all-around transistors using older DUV tools instead of export-restricted EUV equipment; a production-ready node is still years away, but it's a concrete data point in China's push to route around U.S. chip-equipment controls.
