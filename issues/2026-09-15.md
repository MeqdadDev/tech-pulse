# TECH PULSE — SEP 12 – SEP 15, 2026

> Dario Amodei's call to "pace the frontier," quickly endorsed by Sam Altman and Elon Musk, triggered a worldwide AI stock selloff after President Trump dismissed it as a "sick conspiracy," the same week UBTECH fired up the world's first 10,000-unit-scale humanoid robot factory in China.

## AI

- **[Amodei calls to "pace the frontier" — Altman and Musk back him](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/anthropic-ceo-outlines-plan-to-pace-the-frontier/)** — Anthropic's CEO proposed embedding third-party evaluators like METR inside AI labs, domestic safety-standard coordination, and international limits on the most dangerous uses; Altman called it a "good idea" and Musk simply posted "Dario is right." A rare moment of consensus among rival lab chiefs that development pace itself is now a risk to manage.

- **[Trump rejects the slowdown call; chip stocks tumble as hyperscalers gain](https://fortune.com/2026/09/14/ai-slowdown-stocks-nvidia-meta/)** — the President called the warnings a "SICK conspiracy" benefiting China, while Nvidia, Intel, and AMD fell 3–6% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped nearly 6% — even as Microsoft, Alphabet, and Meta gained, since a real slowdown would let them digest existing compute rather than keep buying more.

- **[Microsoft publishes a "Humanist AI" code of conduct](https://microsoft.ai/code-of-conduct/)** — the draft constitution, meant to be trained into future models, bars dangerous cyber assistance, weapons development, deception, and resistance to human shutdown or oversight.

- **[Anthropic signs a $13.7B compute deal with Trump-linked Rum Group](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-makes-13-7-compute-deal-with-trump-linked-rum-group/)** — the six-year cloud deal sent Rumble's stock up 20% and raises fresh conflict-of-interest questions amid the safety-policy sparring.

## Robotics

- **[UBTECH fires up the world's first 10,000-unit-scale humanoid factory](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202609/1370528.shtml)** — the Liuzhou, China plant began production this week, building a Walker S or Cruzr unit roughly every 10 minutes, with robots handling material transport on the line itself.

- **[Unitree open-sources its UnifoLM-WLA-1.0 humanoid foundation model](https://www.humanoidsdaily.com/news/unitree-open-sources-unifolm-wla-1-0-to-tackle-humanoid-generalization)** — the 6B-parameter model runs 64 real-world manipulation tasks from one set of weights with no task-specific fine-tuning, free just as Nvidia's Hugging Face acquisition raises questions about who controls open robotics infrastructure.

- **[Perplexity and Nvidia launch Portable Computer, a fully local AI agent](https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/perplexity-partners-with-nvidia-to-launch-portable-computer-a-fully-local-ai-agent-with-zero-token-costs)** — runs entirely on-device on RTX-equipped Windows PCs at zero per-token cost, another sign agentic software is moving off the API meter.

- **[Revolut confirms a breach via fake government data requests](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/revolut-confirms-customer-data-breach-through-fake-government-requests/)** — attackers impersonating law enforcement got passports, selfies, IBANs, and transaction histories.
