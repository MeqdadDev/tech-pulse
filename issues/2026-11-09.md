# TECH PULSE — NOV 07–09, 2026

> Anthropic locks in a $3.4 billion, 1.7-gigawatt gas-generator deal to power its AI data centers, while Nvidia's board opens an independent investigation into reports that CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence operation.

## AI

- **[Anthropic secures $3.4 billion generator deal for AI data centers, FT reports](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/08/anthropic-generator-deal-ai-data-centers.html)** — Anthropic is paying $3.4B for 1.7GW of Caterpillar gas generators that power-infrastructure firm OnePWR had on order, according to the Financial Times. It's one of the largest known power-generation purchases by any AI lab, and another sign frontier labs are buying their own power plants rather than waiting on the grid.

- **[Statement From NVIDIA Board of Directors](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors)** — Nvidia's board says a committee of independent directors, assisted by outside counsel, is investigating recent reporting that founder and CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. The board says it has no reason to believe national-security or company secrets were compromised, and that Huang is cooperating fully.

## Robotics

- **[Amazon's Astro home robot has fans but looks destined for the 'trash heap.' Why it's trying again](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/08/amazon-home-robot-fauna-sprout.html)** — Amazon has built a new home-robotics team around Fauna Robotics' Sprout humanoid (acquired back in March) to take another shot at a consumer home robot after Astro's lukewarm reception — a sign Amazon isn't giving up on getting a robot into people's living rooms.

## Startups

- **[Seattle's Firmly lands $15M from JPMorgan, Mastercard to power AI shopping bots](https://hoodline.com/2026/11/seattle-s-firmly-lands-15m-from-jpmorgan-mastercard-to-power-ai-shopping-bots)** — Firmly raised a $15M Series A led by Squarepoint Capital, with JPMorgan Payments and Mastercard joining, to help merchants plug their catalogs into AI shopping agents. A concrete sign that serious financial-infrastructure money is now backing the "agentic commerce" plumbing layer.

Startup coverage was otherwise thin this window.

## Hardware

- **[The chip shortage is back, and Microchip says AI customers are crowding everyone else out](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/07/the-chip-shortage-is-back-and-microchip-says-ai-customers-are-crowding-everyone-out/)** — Microchip Technology says AI buyers have taken over third-party chip assembly and test capacity, pushing lead times out from 4–8 weeks to 6–32 weeks for everyone else. The warning sent its shares down roughly 10% and shows the AI chip boom is now squeezing supply for ordinary electronics too.

- **[America's AI spending boom just crossed a staggering $1 trillion milestone](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/08/americas-ai-spending-boom-just-crossed-a-staggering-1-trillion-milestone/)** — US computer and chip imports hit $86.9B in September, an annualized pace of roughly $1.04 trillion. A stark, dollars-and-cents measure of how much hardware the AI buildout is now pulling into the country every year.
