# TECH PULSE — SEP 23 – SEP 25, 2026

> Anthropic's Claude autonomously discovered a novel CRISPR-like enzyme system in bacteriophage DNA, while Qualcomm unveiled dual 2nm Snapdragon chips and agreed to acquire robotics software maker PickNik to push deeper into physical AI.

## AI

- **[Claude discovers a novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system)** — Anthropic says Claude autonomously identified a previously unknown family of enzymes (array-associated reverse transcriptases) in bacteriophage genomes in roughly 21 hours of work, a find CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang called "genuinely intriguing." One of the clearest cases yet of a frontier model making an original scientific discovery rather than summarizing known research.

- **[DeepMind's new chief signals Gemini 4 is coming "as soon as possible"](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/24/google-says-gemini-4-release-is-coming-as-soon-as-possible/)** — Koray Kavukcuoglu indicated Google wants to ship Gemini 4 well ahead of its original year-end target once post-training wraps up, a sign Google feels pressure to keep pace with OpenAI and Anthropic's release cadence.

- **[Amazon opens Seller Central tools to outside AI agents, starting with Claude](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-opens-its-seller-tools-to-outside-ai-agents-starting-with-anthropics-claude/)** — a new plugin lets third-party sellers manage inventory, pricing, and analytics through Claude on Bedrock or Amazon's own Quick assistant, without logging into Seller Central directly — a concrete step toward agentic commerce running one of the world's largest marketplaces.

- **[Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash-Lite TTS models](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-text-to-speech/)** — the new speech models support 100+ languages and 2,000+ voices with prompt-based voice design and finer control over tone and pacing, pushing Google's text-to-speech further ahead on breadth and controllability.

- **[OpenAI releases MentalHealthBench, an open benchmark for AI mental-health conversations](https://www.unite.ai/openai-debuts-mentalhealthbench-for-ai-mental-health-conversations/)** — built from 1,215 conversations reviewed by more than 80 psychologists across 22 countries, the benchmark scored OpenAI's own GPT-6 Astra at just 57.3%, a rare case of a lab publishing a benchmark that makes its flagship model look mediocre.

## Robotics

- **[Qualcomm agrees to acquire PickNik Robotics, will keep MoveIt open source](https://www.therobotreport.com/qualcomm-acquires-picknik-robotics-keep-moveit-open-source/)** — the deal pulls the widely used open-source MoveIt motion-planning framework closer to Qualcomm's Dragonwing robotics chip platform, a bet that owning both the silicon and a dominant piece of open robotics software accelerates its push into physical AI.

- **[IFR: 5 million robots are now working in factories worldwide](https://www.therobotreport.com/5-million-robots-now-working-factories-worldwide-ifr-reports/)** — the International Federation of Robotics reports the global operational stock of industrial robots hit a record 5 million units, up 9%, with China installing 59% of new units in 2025 versus a shrinking share in Europe.

- **[ANYbotics gives its ANYmal quadruped autonomous door access](https://www.therobotreport.com/anybotics-opens-the-door-for-inspections-with-anymal-robots/)** — a partnership with dormakaba and LEGIC lets ANYmal inspection robots badge through access-controlled doors on their own, removing one of the last manual hand-offs limiting how far unattended inspection robots could roam a facility.

- **[Epson launches AX6, its first collaborative six-axis robot](https://www.therobotreport.com/epson-introduces-ax6-cobot-compact-design-no-code-programming/)** — the compact cobot adds no-code programming and rounds out Epson's safety-tool lineup, extending one of the industry's largest arm makers into the fast-growing collaborative-robot category.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[F-Droid 2.0 launches, doubling down on open Android as Google tightens developer rules](https://f-droid.org/2026/09/24/f-droid-2.0-a-new-chapter-for-android-freedom.html)** — the free/open-source Android app repository's biggest release in years arrives just as Google rolls out stricter developer-verification requirements, positioning F-Droid as one of the clearest remaining paths for sideloaded, non-Play-Store Android apps.

- **[Databricks acquires spreadsheet startup Row Zero to bring "governed" AI spreadsheets to Genie](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/databricks-buys-row-zero-and-is-scouting-for-more-startups-to-acquire/)** — the deal lets Databricks' Genie AI assistant read and write live, permission-aware spreadsheets grounded in a company's governed data, and Databricks says it's actively scouting more startups to fold into its platform.

- **[GitHub Copilot ships configurable model-effort tiers and in-container agents](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-github-copilot-weekly-releases-september-14/)** — developers can now pick "efficiency," "balance," or "intelligence" tiers to trade off cost, speed, and quality across VS Code, the CLI, and the Copilot app, while agents gained the ability to run locally inside Dev Containers and open pull requests directly from a session.

- **[A malware impersonator sat unremoved on GitHub for three weeks — until Hacker News noticed](https://successfulsoftware.net/2026/09/24/github-has-not-removed-malicious-imitation-software-after-3-weeks/)** — a fake, malware-laced clone of a legitimate data-wrangling tool sat on GitHub for 23 days despite repeated abuse reports; GitHub pulled it within 10 minutes of the story hitting the Hacker News front page, a pointed reminder to verify software sources rather than trust platform moderation.

## Startups

- **[Tekever closes a $580M Series D at a $6.4B valuation](https://techfundingnews.com/tekever-580m-series-d-6-4b-valuation-uc-investments/)** — the Portuguese drone maker's valuation has nearly quintupled on the back of a fresh £400M UK drone deal, with UC Investments and Baillie Gifford leading and the founder signaling appetite for defense-sector acquisitions.

- **[Snorkel AI triples its valuation to $3.5B as AI training-data demand surges](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/snorkel-ai-triples-valuation-to-3-5b-as-demand-for-ai-training-data-booms/)** — the $350M Series E reflects how much labs now pay for high-quality, well-labeled training data as pretraining scale gains diminishing returns and curated data becomes a bigger competitive lever.

- **[Enveda raises $311M, doubling its valuation to $2B for AI-discovered drugs](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/enveda-secures-311m-to-bring-more-nature-derived-ai-drugs-into-clinical-trials/)** — the round funds more nature-derived drug candidates identified by Enveda's AI platform as they move into human clinical trials, part of a broader wave of capital chasing AI-native drug discovery.

- **[Ando raises a $20M seed for "agent-native" team messaging to challenge Slack](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/ando-eyes-slack-as-it-builds-team-messaging-platform-for-humans-and-agents-to-work-together/)** — Ando's chat platform treats AI agents as first-class participants alongside human coworkers rather than bolted-on bots, betting that as agentic work spreads, the tools people already chat in need to be rebuilt around that shift rather than patched.

## Hardware

- **[Qualcomm's Snapdragon Summit debuts dual 2nm chips that run 30B-parameter models fully offline](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/327920/20260923/qualcomm-snapdragon-summit-debuts-dual-2nm-chips-extreme-gen-6-runs-30b-ai-models-offline.htm)** — the new Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 can run a 30-billion-parameter model entirely on-device, and Qualcomm also brought official Linux support to its Snapdragon X2 PC chips — pushing serious local AI inference and developer-friendly OS support down into phone- and laptop-class silicon.

- **[The newest ESP32 can run full Linux, closing in on Raspberry Pi territory](https://www.xda-developers.com/newest-esp32-run-linux-close-to-raspberry-pi/)** — Espressif's latest microcontroller variant is now capable enough to boot a full Linux distribution, blurring the line between cheap embedded chips and single-board computers for hobbyist and edge-AI projects alike.
