# TECH PULSE — SEP 10 – SEP 13, 2026

> Salesforce shipped seven named Agentforce agents and a new AI Control Plane to govern them, while Swarmer agreed to acquire Ukraine's Ratel Robotics for up to $224M, pairing its swarm-coordination software with combat-tested ground vehicles.

## AI

- **[Salesforce ships seven named, "job-ready" Agentforce agents and an AI Control Plane](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/agentforce-job-ready-ai-agents/)** — Casey, Paige, Carter, Hunter, Marshall, Piper, and Fin run on Customer 360 with existing permissions intact, while the new Control Plane manages agent identity, policy, and cost across models and vendors. Enterprise AI is moving from scattered pilots toward managed fleets of agents with real governance attached.

- **[OpenAI ships GPT-Live-1, a full-duplex voice model, to its API](https://www.unite.ai/openais-gpt-live-1-arrives-in-the-api-at-0-05-per-minute/)** — at $0.05/minute, it listens, speaks, and handles interruptions simultaneously rather than turn-taking, making natural voice agents cheap enough for ordinary products, not just flagship demos.

- **[OpenAI adds a Data agent to ChatGPT Work](https://www.unite.ai/openai-introduces-data-agent-in-chatgpt-work-to-analyze-company-data/)** — employees can query enterprise data and generate dashboards in plain language without writing SQL.

## Robotics

- **[UltraSense launches an ultrasound-based tactile sensing platform for Physical AI](https://www.therobotreport.com/ultrasound-offers-scalable-path-tactile-intelligence-physical-ai/)** — ultrasound instead of resistive/capacitive skins gives robot hands touch perception without the wear-and-tear limiting today's tactile sensors.

- **[Swarmer agrees to acquire Ukraine's Ratel Robotics for up to $224M](https://www.therobotreport.com/swarmer-to-acquire-ukrainian-ugv-maker-ratel-robotics-for-up-to-224m/)** — pairing swarm-coordination software with a leading Ukrainian unmanned-ground-vehicle maker, another sign defense-robotics consolidation is accelerating.

- **[Kinetix AI raises $75M for "ultra-humanoid" embodied robots](https://ventureburn.com/kinetix-ai-raises-75-million-embodied-ai-robotics/)** — led by Temasek's Vertex Ventures, betting that robots built to true human proportions outcompete cheaper, non-humanoid designs.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Bending Spoons agrees to buy Miro for $1.36B](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/10/bending-spoons-to-buy-collaboration-tools-maker-miro-for-1-36b-90-less-than-its-2022-valuation/)** — about 90% below Miro's 2022 valuation, continuing Bending Spoons' spree of picking up once-hot software brands at steep discounts.

- **[Fund Recs launches an agentic platform built on Model Context Protocol](https://funds-europe.com/fund-recs-launches-agentic-ai-platform-for-fund-oversight/)** — a small but concrete example of MCP moving from a dev-tools curiosity into regulated, enterprise-grade software.

## Startups

- **[The Boring Company raises $3B at a $23B valuation, led by UAE investors](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/10/the-boring-company-raises-3b-in-round-led-by-uae/)** — funding 93+ miles of UAE tunnels, a sharp re-rating for Musk's tunneling venture.

- **[Mach Industries doubles its valuation to $3.7B in three months](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/10/defense-tech-mach-industries-doubles-valuation-to-3-7b-in-3-months/)** — a fresh $600M round shows how fast capital is piling into defense-tech manufacturing.

- **[Pentagon in talks to lend AI cloud startup Fluidstack $5B](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/pentagon-in-talks-to-loan-fluidstack-5bn-report/)** — reportedly the largest loan the DoD has ever made, treating AI data-center capacity as national-security infrastructure.

## Hardware

- **[Micron, Samsung, and SK Hynix race to nearly double HBM memory capacity](https://www.sammyfans.com/2026/09/12/micron-doubling-hbm-capacity-as-samsung-sk-hynix-scale-up/)** — all three scrambling to keep pace with AI accelerator demand.

- **[Samsung and Qualcomm's 2nm chip deal stalls over pricing](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/09/11/samsung-qualcomm-2nm-deal/)** — the standoff risks pushing Qualcomm's next-gen processors into 2027.
