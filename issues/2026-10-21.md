# TECH PULSE — OCT 19–21, 2026

> Chinese-linked attackers used autonomous AI agents to breach more than 100 companies in an automated hacking campaign, while ByteDance, Alibaba, and Tencent reportedly ordered more than $35 billion worth of Nvidia's new RTX PRO 5500 chips.

## AI

- **[AI agents turned into cyberweapons breach 100+ companies](https://oodaloop.com/briefs/cyber/chinese-hacker-deployed-anthropic-and-deepseek-and-moonshot-ai-agents-in-massive-100-company-cyberattack/)** — A Chinese-speaking operator reportedly used autonomous AI agents on the open-source Cairn platform against more than 1,200 targets, successfully breaching over 100 companies including John Deere, SharkNinja, and Cummins, at a cost of roughly $50 in model tokens per intrusion. It's one of the starkest demonstrations yet that agentic AI has lowered the cost and skill floor for large-scale hacking.

- **[DHS orders federal agencies to purge DeepSeek AI products](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/19/top-tech-news-today-october-19-2026-anthropic-deepseek-idealab-meta-microsoft-more/)** — The Department of Homeland Security directed all U.S. civilian federal agencies to remove DeepSeek products and any third-party services built on its models, and to block future procurement, under the Federal Acquisition Supply Chain Security Act. It's the clearest federal move yet to wall off Chinese AI models from U.S. government systems.

- **[AstraZeneca and Oracle launch global AI clinical-trial collaboration](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/tech-news-today-october-20-2026-amazon-apple-bytedance-microsoft-nvidia-openai-salesforce-more/)** — The pair will use AI to speed up clinical-trial recruitment and patient identification, starting with a hypertension-care pilot in Abu Dhabi alongside healthcare group M42. Terms are a non-binding agreement, but it signals growing pharma-cloud AI partnerships aimed at trial bottlenecks.

## Robotics

- **[Gecko Robotics secures $300M U.S. Navy contract](https://www.therobotreport.com/robotics-news/)** — The Pittsburgh robotics company landed a new $300 million Navy deal for AI-powered ship inspection and maintenance, which Gecko says reflects the Pentagon treating autonomous inspection robots as "a core military capability that is delivering results."

- **[Daimon Robotics builds a tactile-intelligence infrastructure layer](https://www.therobotreport.com/robotics-news/)** — The startup is linking tactile sensors, real-world data collection, simulation, and AI models so robot hands can reliably handle delicate physical tasks, pushing "physical AI" closer to human-level dexterity.

- **[Avenda Health adds intraoperative Surgery Mode to its Unfold AI platform](https://www.therobotreport.com/robotics-news/)** — The new mode gives surgeons patient-specific 3D cancer maps in real time during robotic prostate procedures, aiming to improve precision in a surgery where visualizing tumor margins is notoriously difficult.

- **[Kodama raises $7M to bring autonomy to forestry equipment](https://www.therobotreport.com/robotics-news/)** — The startup is automating logging and forestry machinery, starting with teleoperation to collect the real-world data it needs to eventually run equipment autonomously in rugged terrain.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[AWS open-sources TorchNeuron, a PyTorch backend for its AI chips](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/tech-news-today-october-20-2026-amazon-apple-bytedance-microsoft-nvidia-openai-salesforce-more/)** — The new backend lets developers run native PyTorch on Trainium2 and Trainium3, entering public beta October 20. It's AWS's latest push to make its custom silicon a drop-in alternative to Nvidia GPUs for training and inference.

- **[Microsoft expands Guardian Agents to police rogue AI agents](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/tech-news-today-october-20-2026-amazon-apple-bytedance-microsoft-nvidia-openai-salesforce-more/)** — The Project Perception and Microsoft 365 E7 expansion lets admins detect, restrict, or shut down AI agents in real time, with local discovery now covering 60 tool types — including Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, and GitHub Copilot — and task-scoped permissions that expire automatically.

- **[F5 rushes out-of-band fixes for three critical NGINX flaws](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/tech-news-today-october-20-2026-amazon-apple-bytedance-microsoft-nvidia-openai-salesforce-more/)** — The patches address CVE-2026-15176, CVE-2026-97205, and CVE-2026-93479. Worst-case remote-code-execution scenarios need extra conditions (like disabled ASLR), and F5 hasn't confirmed active exploitation — but teams running NGINX should patch promptly.

- **[Supply-chain attack hits WordPress's Rank Math PRO plugin](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/tech-news-today-october-20-2026-amazon-apple-bytedance-microsoft-nvidia-openai-salesforce-more/)** — A compromised build (version 3.0.123) of the popular SEO plugin carried code that could grant persistent attacker access, distributed October 15–16. A clean version (3.0.124) is out; affected sites should restore from pre-incident backups.

## Hardware

- **[Chinese tech giants order $35B+ of Nvidia's new RTX PRO 5500 chips](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/tech-news-today-october-20-2026-amazon-apple-bytedance-microsoft-nvidia-openai-salesforce-more/)** — ByteDance, Alibaba, and Tencent have reportedly ordered more than 3 million of Nvidia's RTX PRO 5500 cards, but Nvidia has warned it can't guarantee full delivery — some buyers may get only 5–10% of what they ordered, with shipments starting Q1 2027.

- **[Nvidia's new Vera CPUs land their first major cloud customer](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/tech-news-today-october-20-2026-amazon-apple-bytedance-microsoft-nvidia-openai-salesforce-more/)** — AI cloud startup Modal is adopting Nvidia's Vera processors, reporting roughly double the throughput in its own tests; Nvidia is targeting at least $20 billion in Vera revenue this fiscal year.

- **[Amazon to invest over $18B in Germany, including a new Berlin robotics/AI lab](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/tech-news-today-october-20-2026-amazon-apple-bytedance-microsoft-nvidia-openai-salesforce-more/)** — The 2027 investment expands the AWS Frankfurt region by roughly $5.4 billion and opens a second Amazon robotics and AI research lab in Berlin, deepening Amazon's European AI and logistics footprint.

- **[Apple commits $100M to U.S. titanium production](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/tech-news-today-october-20-2026-amazon-apple-bytedance-microsoft-nvidia-openai-salesforce-more/)** — The three-year deal with Perryman Company supports domestic titanium supply, part of Apple's broader push to source iPhone components — including camera films for the iPhone 18 Pro from Qnity Electronics — from suppliers in more than a dozen U.S. states.
