# TECH PULSE — NOV 09–11, 2026

> Anthropic priced its IPO at up to a $1.4 trillion valuation just as Nvidia launched GR00T 2 and Isaac Lab 3.0 at CoRL to push humanoid robots further out of simulation and into the real world.

## AI

- **[Anthropic prices IPO at up to a $1.404 trillion valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/)** — Anthropic set its IPO range at $70–$78 a share, which would make it one of the largest tech listings ever; all seven founders are set to cash out roughly $250M each when it prices. The debut, timed before Thanksgiving, will be a real-time test of how public markets price frontier AI labs.

- **[Anthropic introduces Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)** — The two models are pitched as Anthropic's most capable yet for demanding technical work, arriving the same week as the IPO roadshow. Worth a look for anyone benchmarking frontier coding/science performance against current tools.

- **[Anthropic launches Claude Meetings](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/)** — A new feature that sits in on workplace meetings and turns the discussion directly into plans and assigned tasks for Claude agents to execute. It's a concrete step toward "ambient" agentic workflows rather than chat-only assistants.

## Robotics

- **[NVIDIA launches GR00T 2 and Isaac Lab 3.0 at CoRL](https://www.therobotreport.com/nvidia-launches-gr00t-2-isaac-lab-3-0-corl/)** — Unveiled at the Conference on Robot Learning, the updated foundation model and simulation toolkit are aimed squarely at training humanoid and industrial robots faster with less real-world data. This is the platform a large share of the humanoid ecosystem builds on top of, so the update ripples widely.

- **[Boston Dynamics adds wheels to Atlas humanoid to test whole-body control](https://www.therobotreport.com/boston-dynamics-adds-wheels-to-atlas-humanoid-to-test-whole-body-control/)** — A wheeled variant of Atlas is being used to push whole-body control research, blending legged and wheeled locomotion in one control stack. It's an R&D probe rather than a product, but it signals where Boston Dynamics sees mobility research heading next.

- **[Dynamic Creatures partners with Sixth Wall Studio to bring Hasbro characters to life with robots](https://www.therobotreport.com/dynamic-creatures-partners-sixth-wall-studio-bring-hasbro-characters-life-with-robots/)** — The partnership will use Hasbro's new AI character-licensing studio to build animatronic/robotic versions of toy IP. A smaller story, but a useful signal of consumer robotics and licensed-character AI starting to merge.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[GitHub Copilot adds Gemini 4 Carbon and Claude Fable 5.2 as selectable models](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-10-gemini-4-carbon-in-github-copilot)** — Copilot added Google's Gemini 4 Carbon (Nov 10) on the heels of Claude Fable 5.2 (Nov 9), continuing GitHub's multi-model strategy inside the IDE. Same week, GitHub also shipped a C++ language server that auto-configures MSBuild builds, secret scanning for GitHub Actions workflow logs, and a revamped dashboard.

- **[N-able takes its remote access service offline after researcher discloses dozens of zero-day bugs](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/n-able-takes-its-remote-access-service-offline-after-researcher-publishes-dozens-of-zero-day-bugs/)** — The IT management vendor pulled its remote-access product after a security researcher published a large batch of unpatched vulnerabilities. Worth noting for anyone running N-able tooling, or relying on similar remote-management stacks, as a reminder to check vendor advisories closely this week.

- **[Cyberattacks threaten to make open-source AI "a little less open," Hugging Face exec warns](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/cyberattacks-threaten-to-make-open-source-ai-a-little-less-open-hugging-face-exec-warns/)** — A Hugging Face executive flagged rising attacks on open model repositories as a pressure pushing some labs toward more gated releases. A trend worth watching for teams that depend on fully open weights and permissive licensing.

## Startups

- **[Oura to acquire AI-native primary care startup Counsel Health for $625M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/oura-to-acquire-ai-native-primary-care-startup-counsel-health-for-625m/)** — The wearables maker is buying an AI-driven primary-care startup, extending its push from fitness tracking into actual clinical care. It's one of the largest digital-health acquisitions of the past few months.

- **[OpenAI acquires travel startup Odessia](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-acquires-odessia-travel-startup-created-by-sonder-founder/)** — OpenAI picked up Odessia, a travel-planning startup founded by the former Sonder CEO, continuing its pattern of tucking vertical-specific startups directly into ChatGPT's product surface.

- **[AI hardware startup Nominal triples valuation to $3B in 8 months](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/ai-hardware-startup-nominal-triples-valuation-to-3b-in-8-months/)** — Nominal's hardware-engineering and test platform jumped from a $1B valuation in March to $3B now, a sign investors are still pricing AI-for-hardware tooling aggressively even as overall deal counts tighten.

## Hardware

- **[Pixel Tag, Google's $30 Android-ready answer to Apple's AirTag, is now available](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/pixel-tag-googles-30-android-ready-answer-to-apples-airtag-is-now-available/)** — Google's UWB-equipped item tracker, first unveiled months ago, has now actually shipped and is available to buy. It directly targets Apple's AirTag with deeper Android Find Hub integration.

- **[Intel and Altera expand collaboration to simplify sensor integration for physical AI](https://www.therobotreport.com/intel-altera-expand-collaboration-simplify-sensor-integration-physical-ai/)** — The expanded partnership focuses on making it easier to wire sensors into physical-AI and robotics chips, an unglamorous but important piece of the stack for anyone building perception-heavy robots.

- **[X-energy gets green light to build its first small modular nuclear reactors](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/x-energy-gets-green-light-to-build-its-first-small-modular-nuclear-reactors/)** — X-energy cleared a key approval to begin construction on its first small modular reactors, part of the broader scramble to secure dedicated power for AI data centers.
