# TECH PULSE — NOV 11 – NOV 13, 2026

> Nvidia's board ousted founder-CEO Jensen Huang after a foreign-intelligence probe, while Anthropic wraps its roadshow ahead of pricing what could be the second-largest IPO in history this week.

## AI

- **[Nvidia's board ousts Jensen Huang as CEO after espionage probe](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm)** — Nvidia announced on November 12 that founder Jensen Huang resigned as President, CEO and board member, effective immediately, following a board investigation into reports he was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. CFO Colette Kress becomes interim CEO with no permanent successor named yet — a major leadership shock at the company supplying most of the world's AI compute, days before its Q3 earnings call.

- **[Anthropic nears Nasdaq pricing in one of the largest IPOs ever](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/122657/anthropic-sets-terms-for-61-billion-ipo-targeting-a-1.2-trillion-market-cap)** — Anthropic set terms for a roughly $61B offering at a $1.2 trillion valuation, with pricing expected November 13 and trading to begin the week of November 16 under ticker ANT — second only to SpaceX's 2026 debut in IPO size.

- **[DeepMind launches AlphaGenome 2 for DNA variant prediction](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/deepmind-launches-alphagenome-2-for-dna-variant-prediction)** — Google DeepMind released a new DNA sequence model trained on over 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues, nearly doubling its predecessor's accuracy at identifying disease-causing genetic variants (30% vs. 16% on a key immune-cell benchmark).

- **[TSMC to invest $1B in Anthropic and OpenAI ahead of IPO](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-11-11)** — TSMC's board approved splitting roughly $500M each into OpenAI preferred stock and Anthropic common shares at IPO price, deepening the circular financing between chipmakers and the AI labs that consume their output — a dynamic analysts say complicates reading real demand.

- **[Anthropic research: single shared agents outperform multi-agent teams](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-11-12)** — A new Anthropic alignment paper, "Worse Together," found that when each person on a team delegates to their own AI agent rather than sharing one, group outcomes got worse across every one of four tested multi-agent environments — a notable caution against the industry's push toward personal agent swarms.

## Robotics

- **[Google DeepMind launches Gemini Robotics 2.5](https://www.siliconreport.com/google-deepmind-introduces-gemini-robotics-2-5)** — DeepMind's newest vision-language-action model handles hundreds of tasks from folding laundry to sorting car parts, with 20–30% faster task completion and up to 100% better success rates on delicate manipulation tasks than its predecessor; it's now rolling out to robotics partners.

Beyond that, little else cleared the high-impact bar for robotics in this short window — worth another look next cycle.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[OpenAI cuts off Cursor's model access November 12](https://ai-tldr.dev/releases/org/openai/)** — OpenAI's model-supply deal with Cursor ends today, following SpaceX's acquisition of the coding-tool maker — a sign of frontier labs tightening control over who gets to build on their models as competition for developer mindshare intensifies.

- **[Browser makers ship "Modern Web Guidance" for coding agents](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-11-11)** — Chrome, Edge, Firefox and MDN jointly released a one-line-install package that feeds coding agents current web-platform best practices, arguing that LLMs default to outdated patterns because their training data is full of them.

- **[Microsoft: fewer than 10 neurons can backdoor an AI coding agent](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-11-11)** — Microsoft Research disclosed "Lazarus," a technique that backdoors an LLM agent by reprogramming under 10 dormant neurons with no additional training — the triggered model can quietly execute attacker code while passing standard safety evaluations, a serious concern for open-weight model provenance.

- **[Hugging Face adds Decision Models to Transformers](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-11-11)** — Instead of asking an LLM to generate JSON, a new model class takes a state and fixed set of options and returns a probability distribution directly — aimed at making routing and triage tasks in agent pipelines cheaper and more reliable.

## Startups

- **[Uforce raises $700M at $6B valuation for combat-tested AI drones](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-11-12)** — The Ukrainian defense-tech firm's round, co-led by Valor Equity Partners and the UK's National Wealth Fund, jumps its valuation 6x from roughly $1B in March. Uforce builds AI-coordinated aerial, maritime and land systems with over 300,000 combat flights logged in Ukraine.

Otherwise a quiet window for startup news specifically — most of the oxygen this week went to Nvidia's leadership shakeup and Anthropic's IPO roadshow.

## Hardware

- **[Apple's HomeView smart home hub ships November 13](https://www.popsci.com/gear/apple-homeview-homepod-tv-4k-smart-home-devices-details/)** — Apple's first new product category in years, a $299 Siri-powered smart display, launches today alongside a fourth-generation Apple TV 4K, closing out the company's busiest year of hardware launches.
