# TECH PULSE — SEP 03 – SEP 04, 2026

> OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra becomes the first model to cross a "Critical" cybersecurity capability threshold, while Figure signs a $3.5B deal with Nscale for up to 100,000 Nvidia GPUs to power its next-generation humanoid robots.

## AI

- **[OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra, its first "Critical"-tier cyber model](https://openai.com/index/safety-overview-gpt-6-astra/)** — the model scored 100% on ExploitBench and can autonomously find and develop exploits, so OpenAI is gating access, pricing it at $10–$50 per million tokens, and blocking proof-of-concept exploit requests. It's the clearest signal yet that frontier labs now treat some capabilities as too dangerous to ship unrestricted.

- **[Nvidia confirms $12.93B acquisition of Hugging Face](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-it-will-buy-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion/)** — the deal hands Nvidia the platform hosting 3M+ models and 18M developers, though Nvidia says it will keep the hub open to AMD and other hardware. Puts the default distribution layer for open AI models under a single chipmaker's control.

- **[Meta ships Muse Spark 1.3](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-1-3)** — An agentic coding model using ~20% fewer tool calls and ~25% fewer tokens than its predecessor on the same long-horizon tasks — the coding-agent race is increasingly about efficiency, not just raw scores.

- **[Google releases Gemini 3.8 Flash and a gated "Flash Cyber" variant](https://gbhackers.com/google-unveils-gemini-3-8-flash-cyber-for-autonomous-vulnerability/)** — Restricted to vetted defenders, patching vulnerabilities 2.6x faster than rival tools — Google, like OpenAI this same week, is fencing off its most capable security tooling rather than releasing it broadly.

## Robotics

- **[Figure signs $3.5B compute deal with Nscale](https://www.humanoidsdaily.com/news/figure-inks-multi-billion-dollar-compute-deal-with-nscale-to-deploy-100-000-next-gen-nvidia-gpus)** — For up to 100,000 next-gen Nvidia GPUs, with Nscale taking an equity stake and the deal potentially scaling past $6B. Notable because Figure has raised only $1.9B total to date — humanoid training now demands hyperscaler-level compute even pre-volume-shipment.

- **[IFA Berlin 2026 turns into an embodied-AI showcase](https://www.forbes.com/sites/michaelashley/2026/09/04/at-ifa-2026-ai-finally-gets-a-body/)** — MagicLab's VLA-model humanoids, D-Robotics' home-robot computing platforms, and OlloBot's companion robot all debuted. Consumer robotics is visibly moving from novelty demo to a coordinated product category with dedicated software and silicon.

## Software / Dev Tools

- **[Microsoft unveils Project Zenith](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsdeveloper/2026/09/04/announcing-project-zenith-the-ready-to-code-windows-experience/)** — A "ready-to-code" Windows configuration for developer-class PCs (64GB+ RAM, AMD Ryzen) that runs 30B+ parameter AI models locally out of the box. Positions Windows as a local-inference platform for coding agents, not just a cloud-API client.

- **[Snowflake lifts FY2027 revenue guidance to $6.07B](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/03/top-tech-news-today-september-3-2026-google-hugging-face-meta-moonshot-ai-nvidia-more/)** — On 37% product-revenue growth driven by AI workloads — concrete evidence enterprise AI spend is converting into durable infrastructure revenue.

- **[Adobe names Anil Chakravarthy as CEO](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/04/top-tech-news-today-september-4-2026-amazon-google-microsoft-nvidia-openai-tesla-more/)** — Effective December 1 — a leadership reset explicitly framed around navigating generative AI's disruption of creative software.

## Startups

- **[Crusoe raises $3B+ at a $30B valuation](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/04/startup-funding-news-today-september-4-2026-crusoe-gimlet-labs-hivebotics-more/)** — triple its worth of 10 months ago — on custom-GPU supply deals with Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI, plus a reported $13B, five-year cloud agreement with Jane Street.

- **[Gimlet Labs raises $300M at a $3B valuation](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/04/startup-funding-news-today-september-4-2026-crusoe-gimlet-labs-hivebotics-more/)** — Also tripling in six months, for software that spreads AI workloads across chip architectures — a bet that chip-agnostic infrastructure gets more valuable as custom silicon proliferates.

- **[HubX raises a $75M Series A at a $1.2B valuation](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/03/venture-capital-startup-funding-roundup-september-3-2026-base10-partners-gradient-ventures-point72-y-combinator-more/)** — For its AI-optimized mobile-app holding-company model, led by Point72 — a large, quietly-built valuation few outside Turkey have been tracking.

- **[Hivebotics raises a $6M Series A](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/04/startup-funding-news-today-september-4-2026-crusoe-gimlet-labs-hivebotics-more/)** — For autonomous robots that clean commercial restrooms — robotics investment reaching into unglamorous, high-labor-turnover service niches, not just humanoids.

## Hardware

- **[Broadcom's AI chip revenue triples to $16.7B](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326405/20260903/broadcom-custom-ai-chip-revenue-surges-221-167b-q4-guidance-disappoints.htm)** — With custom silicon now ~73% of its AI sales and a stated path to $230B in AI chip revenue by fiscal 2028 — the clearest earnings-level evidence yet that custom ASICs are eating into Nvidia's share of AI compute.

- **[Trezor discloses a third-party breach now affecting 67,000 more U.S. customers](https://cyberinsider.com/trezor-says-data-of-another-67000-us-customers-exposed-in-breach/)** — Exposing 2019–2021 order data via shipping partner ShipMonk — a reminder that hardware-wallet security depends on the whole supply chain, not just device firmware.
